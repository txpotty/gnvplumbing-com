    A whole-home PEX repipe usually costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in usually run $800 to $2,500.

    Signs you might need PEX repiping include consistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are typical in older Smyrna homes and point to a failing plumbing system.

    PEX is a good fit for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing. Humidity causes condensation on cold water lines and toilet tanks, which can quietly damage floors and encourage mold. While rigid pipes can burst when water freezes, PEX can expand and contract, greatly lowering the risk of burst pipes during cold snaps. It also performs well in high temperatures, making it a reliable choice year-round in Smyrna.

    PEX piping is designed to be highly durable and has an estimated lifespan of 50 years or more. Its resistance to corrosion, scaling and freeze damage adds to its long-term reliability, making it a smart investment for your Smyrna home.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light degrades it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or completely covered.
