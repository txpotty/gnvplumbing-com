    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Smyrna, DE

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method.     Slab Leak Reroute in Smyrna

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is easiest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Smyrna, DE

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This lets our plumbers reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but calls for skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Smyrna, DE.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Smyrna, DE
