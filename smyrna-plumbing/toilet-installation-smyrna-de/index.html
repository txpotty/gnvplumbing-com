    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Smyrna

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. An access plate hides the tank. The carrier holds the whole load, so its feet get anchored to slab or framing and it gets plumbed before any wall finish goes on. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Smyrna

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Smyrna, DE

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first.     Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Smyrna

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. A T-valve splits water off the fill line.     Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Smyrna

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. They recover instantly between flushes.
