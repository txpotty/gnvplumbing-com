    Efficient PEX Repiping in Smyrna, DE

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Smyrna, DE. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.
