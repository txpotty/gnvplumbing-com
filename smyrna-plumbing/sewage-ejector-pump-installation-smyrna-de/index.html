    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Smyrna

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. Never mix the two.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Smyrna

    Macerating systems like Saniflo add a bath fixture or two without breaking the concrete floor. The unit sits behind the toilet, grinds the waste and pumps it through a small discharge line, often three-quarter inch or one inch, up to the nearest drain, which makes it a good fit for a small basement half bath. Venting still applies.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Smyrna

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor.
