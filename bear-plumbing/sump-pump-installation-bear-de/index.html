    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Bear

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume.     Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Bear

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go.     Primary Sump Pump Installation in Bear

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Bear

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. Then we patch the slab. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Bear, DE

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. They make matching backup units.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Bear, DE

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. Finished basements get the most out of them.
