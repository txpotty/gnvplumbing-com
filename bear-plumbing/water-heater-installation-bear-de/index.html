    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Bear

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater typically costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installations often run $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and venting needs.

    To improve efficiency, make sure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and cut energy consumption.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is wise to have a professional inspection.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, often breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more cost-effective choice. Newer models also offer significantly better energy efficiency.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve or check valve, an expansion tank is typically required. It protects your water heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    Most standard water heater installations, particularly for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring significant plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater installation involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity and water connections, all of which carry serious risks if not handled correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, gas explosions, electrical fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It can also void your warranty, and local codes typically require a licensed plumber for this work.

    GNV Plumbing installs all major reputable brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as many sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.
