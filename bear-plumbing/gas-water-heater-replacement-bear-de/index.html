    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Big households see payback sooner.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Bear

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. With a check or regulator on the main, expanding hot water has nowhere to go and the pressure spikes each heating cycle. Code commonly calls for an expansion tank. We set its air charge to match house pressure.

    No Hot Water Calls in Bear

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. We check those first. On a tank past ten years, a new heater usually beats a new valve.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Bear

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Delaware homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Current tanks are often taller and wider. We check clearance before we order. Most of these swaps take two to three hours.

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter.
