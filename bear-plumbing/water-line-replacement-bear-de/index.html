    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Bear, DE

    Polybutylene pipe was used in some homes built in the 1980s and 1990s. It can become brittle and fail with no warning. Many insurers list it as a risk. We replace polybutylene service lines with durable materials. This lowers the chance of sudden leaks.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Bear

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a popular and cost-effective choice for water line replacement in Bear. It's flexible, resistant to freezing, and less prone to scaling and corrosion than some traditional materials. Its flexibility allows for simpler installation, often requiring fewer connections, which reduces the potential for leaks. PEX is also a good option for homes with corrosive soil conditions, offering a reliable alternative to metal pipes.

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Bear

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and discolored water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often immediate.
