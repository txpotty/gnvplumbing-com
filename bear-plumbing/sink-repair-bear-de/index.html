    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Bear, DE

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Bear

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. We free the flywheel from underneath with a 1/4 inch hex key, press the red reset button, and check for a bent impeller or a broken grinding plate. Leaks are a different story. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.
