    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Bear

    Sand kills submersibles early. Grit in a well chews up impellers fast, so we look for it on the old pump and may set the new one higher or add a sand separator. Pump setting depth matters.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Bear

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. Depending on depth and pump weight, it gets replaced with schedule 120 PVC or poly. Wire gets the same look. Cracked insulation and taped splices get cut out.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Bear, DE

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. On a three-wire submersible, the start capacitor or the relay in that box fails far more often than the motor down the well does. Those get tested first. Swapping a capacitor costs a fraction of pulling a pump.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Bear, DE

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. A pinhole in the suction pipe lets air in, the pump loses prime, and it runs dry until the seal burns up. We pressure test the line. If the well is too deep for a jet pump, we will say so and quote a submersible.

    Well Pump Sizing in Bear

    Sizing starts with the well log. We get depth and static level from the well log or a sounder, then a draw-down test shows what the well can really produce in gallons per minute. Pumps bigger than the well can supply run dry.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Bear

    Franklin SubDrive and Grundfos SQE are the two constant pressure systems we install most. Both use a small 2 to 20 gallon tank instead of a big one, since the drive keeps the pump running at a matched speed. The soft start is easier on the motor. The upfront cost is higher.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Bear

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. If the pump clicks on every time someone runs a faucet for ten seconds, the bladder in the tank has probably failed and the pump is short cycling itself to death. A tire gauge on the air valve tells us a lot. Precharge should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure.

    What a Well Pump Job Costs in Bear, DE

    A well pump job usually runs a few thousand dollars, and the pump itself is often less than half of that. The rest is labor, wire, pipe and fittings, plus the hoist time to get it all down the hole. Every line is on the quote.
