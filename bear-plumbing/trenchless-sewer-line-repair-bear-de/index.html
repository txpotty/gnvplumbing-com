    Pipe Bursting in Bear, DE

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. The new HDPE pipe is butt-fused into one length first.     Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in Bear, DE

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. That is called channel rot. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, that beats jackhammering.

    Sectional Point Repairs in Bear

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. It fixes a single crack or root entry, not a whole failing line. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.
