    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Bear, DE

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. Redundancy matters here.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Bear

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Bear, DE

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.
