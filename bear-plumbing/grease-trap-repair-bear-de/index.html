    Residential Grease Trap Repair in Bear, DE

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Bear

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. When that wall spalls, grease slips through to the second compartment.     Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Bear, DE

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. The common rule is to pump when grease and solids fill a quarter of the liquid depth.
