    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Sewer Line in Bear

    The cost to clear a clogged sewer line in Bear typically ranges from $250 to $800, depending on the severity and location of the clog, and the method required (e.g., snaking versus hydro-jetting). More complex issues like extensive root removal or pipe damage could increase the cost.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful method where high-pressure water is blasted through your sewer lines to scour the pipe walls clean. It thoroughly removes grease, mineral buildup, scale, and even tree roots, leaving your pipes in a near-new condition. It's much more effective than traditional snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs.

    Cutting roots clears the line but roots will grow back if the joints remain open. Root treatments can slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement seals the joints for good. We can show you the extent of root intrusion on camera.

    Most main line clearings take one to two hours. Severe root intrusion or lines without a cleanout can take longer. Camera inspection adds roughly 30 to 60 minutes.
