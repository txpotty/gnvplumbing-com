    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Bear

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. A new cartridge fixes it. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Bear, DE

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. The head comes off first. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Bear, DE

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Bear, DE

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Bear

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. When that happens, water goes under the shower instead of down the pipe, and the first sign is often a soft spot in the floor next to the shower or a stain in the ceiling below. The drain gets cleared and tested. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Bear

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. A new cartridge fixes it. If you cannot get the water hot enough, the anti-scald limit stop may just need adjusting.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Bear, DE

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.
