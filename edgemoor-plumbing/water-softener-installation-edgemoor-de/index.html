    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Edgemoor

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Then we program it. We set regeneration overnight.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Edgemoor

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Maintenance is light. They work best on moderate water.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Edgemoor, DE

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. GNV Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.

    Water Softener Replacement in Edgemoor, DE

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. You will notice soft water the same day.
