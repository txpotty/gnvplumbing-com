    While there will be some disruption, GNV Plumbing strives to minimize it. We take precautions to protect your property, cover furniture, and clean up completely. We often complete the work in sections to maintain some water access during the project.

    We use high-quality, industry-leading brands for our replacement materials, such as Uponor AquaPEX or Viega PEX for PEX systems, and reputable manufacturers for copper piping. We make sure all materials meet or exceed industry standards for durability and performance.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A and Type L copper with Viega ProPress or soldered fittings. We recommend materials based on your home and water.

    Both PEX and copper are strong choices. PEX is flexible, less expensive, more freeze-resistant, and handles ground movement better than rigid pipe. Copper offers superior rigidity, a long lifespan, and a strong track record. The 'better' choice depends on your budget, preferences, and home's particular needs.

    Look for gray, flexible plastic pipe at your water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff, often stamped PB2110. Homes built from the late 1970s to mid 1990s are most likely to have it.
