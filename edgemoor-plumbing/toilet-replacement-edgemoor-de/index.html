    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Edgemoor

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in.     Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Edgemoor

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. If the floor is uneven, we shim it level, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Edgemoor

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. It gets checked on every replacement. A split ring gets a stainless repair ring, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Edgemoor, DE

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models. Getting one up a staircase takes two people, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Toilet Brands We Replace in Edgemoor

    Brand matters less than model, because every major maker sells a great toilet and a mediocre one at nearly the same price. We look at the flush valve size and MaP score.     Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Edgemoor, DE

Offset flanges can fix a small mismatch.
