    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Edgemoor

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but avoid hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Fleck Water Softener Replacement in Edgemoor, DE

    Fleck is another leading brand known for durable water softener components. When your Fleck system needs replacing in Edgemoor, DE, our plumbers are experienced with these units. We will carefully remove the old Fleck valve and tanks, install the new equipment and program it to your water hardness and usage patterns. This restores efficient water softening.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Edgemoor, DE

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Edgemoor

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Edgemoor, DE

    For homeowners in Edgemoor, DE looking for an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they reliably protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you transition from a salt-based unit.

    Softener Loop Installation in Edgemoor, DE

    Many homes have a softener loop near the main water line that allows easy installation. When a home lacks one, we can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service easier.
