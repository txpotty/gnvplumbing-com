    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Edgemoor, DE

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. New PEX or copper takes its place. Most homeowners notice the pressure difference the first time they turn on the shower.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Edgemoor, DE

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. Copper commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than PEX, and some owners prefer it anyway for its long history. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Edgemoor, DE

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Edgemoor

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved. We coordinate with the HOA, schedule water shutoffs that may affect neighbors and protect adjoining units from dust and noise as much as we can. Building-wide jobs go in phases. For larger buildings, we repipe one stack or wing at a time so residents can stay in place.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Edgemoor, DE

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. We replace the full system with PEX or copper. Many insurers ask about it.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Edgemoor

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. We count fixtures, check attic and crawlspace access, look at where the main comes in and ask about plans for future remodels before we draw a route. Then we size the lines. A three-bath house with a tankless heater may need 1-inch main trunk lines to keep showers steady when the washer kicks on.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Edgemoor

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Edgemoor

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.
