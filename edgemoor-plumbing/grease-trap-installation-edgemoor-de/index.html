    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Edgemoor, DE

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks typically range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers. Sandy soil drains quickly but can shift and settle, which may misalign buried sewer lines and water service pipes over time. We plan excavation equipment around the ground conditions we find on site. A properly installed interceptor can go much longer between pump-outs than an indoor trap.

    Automatic grease removal devices skim grease from the water and collect it in a container your staff empties.     Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in Edgemoor, DE

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Edgemoor

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with limited space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    Grease Trap Installation for Schools and Institutions in Edgemoor

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Edgemoor, DE

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens.
