    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Milford, DE

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are loud. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Milford

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. If the floor is uneven, we shim it level, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Milford, DE

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more. A WaterSense model uses 1.28 gallons or less, and the better ones still clear the bowl in one try because the trapway and flush valve are larger than they used to be. Savings show up fast. Check with your water provider about rebates.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Milford

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. At that point a swap makes more sense than another repair. We pull the old toilet, scrape the flange and check it for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Milford, DE

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. Clearances get checked before we order anything. Sensor flush valves are an easy add during the swap, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.

    Toilet Replacement Cost Comparison in Milford, DE

    Three repair calls in a year on the same toilet usually cost more than replacing it, especially when the toilet is old enough to waste water on every flush. We put both numbers side by side. The decision is yours, and we do not push replacement on a toilet that only needs a flapper.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Milford

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. The drain location stays the same. Pair it with a grab bar if you can, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Milford

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.
