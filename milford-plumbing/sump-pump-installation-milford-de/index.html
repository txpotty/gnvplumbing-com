    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Milford, DE

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is a concern. Our team is experienced in installing these pumps, making sure of proper pit depth and discharge line setup for optimal performance in Milford, DE.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Milford, DE

    Strong storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out. We install backup systems with sealed batteries and charging units. Many include alarms that alert you to problems. Backups add an extra layer of protection for finished basements.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Milford

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, running quietly and taking up less space. They are typically more powerful and durable, making them a popular choice for many homeowners. We handle the complete installation of submersible units, including proper sealing and electrical connections, matched to the specific needs of your Milford property.
