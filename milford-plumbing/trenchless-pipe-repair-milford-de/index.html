    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Milford

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Milford yard.

    Sectional Point Repair in Milford

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. A spot liner fixes that. Guided by camera, a short liner is placed over the bad section and cured, so you pay for a few feet instead of the full line. It suits isolated damage in Milford laterals.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Milford

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. We inflate an epoxy-soaked liner against the wall and cure it with heat or UV, leaving a smooth pipe with no joints. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.
