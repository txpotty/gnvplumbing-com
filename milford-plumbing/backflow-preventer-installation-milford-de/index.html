    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Milford, DE, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device.     Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Milford

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines.
