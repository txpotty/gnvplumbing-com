    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Milford

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where appropriate.

    Drain Field Clog Remediation in Milford

    A clogged drain field is a frequent cause of septic system backups and can be a serious issue for homeowners in Milford. This can be caused by excessive solids, tree root intrusion or soil compaction. We offer professional drain field remediation, which may include hydro-jetting the distribution lines, addressing root infiltration or assessing the soil's percolation capacity to restore proper effluent absorption and prevent future flooding.

    Effluent Pump Repair in Milford, DE

    Homes with uphill or distant fields often use an effluent pump in a pump tank. Failed pumps, stuck floats or tripped breakers set off high water alarms. We test the pump, float switches and control panel and replace failed parts. A working alarm gives you warning before sewage comes back into the house.
