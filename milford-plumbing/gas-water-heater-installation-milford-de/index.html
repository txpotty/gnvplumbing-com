    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Milford, DE

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust.     Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Milford, DE

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. That load usually calls for a 3/4 inch gas line or larger and a dedicated vent in PVC or stainless steel, depending on whether the unit is condensing. They never run out. Private wells are common in rural Delaware, and they can carry iron, nitrates, or low pH that calls for testing and treatment. A scale filter may be worth adding.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Milford

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. Most conversions need a bigger gas line from the meter, a new vent through the wall or roof, a condensate drain and isolation valves for future descaling. Plan on a full day. We do all of it to code.
