    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Milford

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Milford, DE

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500. Sound tank and valve, repair wins. An obsolete valve with scarce parts tips the decision toward a new head or a new unit.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Milford, DE

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. GNV Plumbing repairs these units in Milford, DE. The media wears out in a few years.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Milford, DE

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. GNV Plumbing gives honest advice based on both. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. You get a clear breakdown.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Milford

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. New resin restores full capacity.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Milford

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. We test each part. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.
