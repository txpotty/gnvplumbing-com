    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Milford, DE

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We replace the pump and float rod and rerun the PVC up to the check valve. Cheaper than a submersible, too.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Milford, DE

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We replace like for like or upsize. Sewage ejectors and grinder pumps we quote separately.

    Emergency Sump Pump Swaps in Milford, DE

    Pit filling, pump not running? Call (302) 406-6354 and we will send someone. Keep the lid off, unplug anything sitting on the wet floor, and use a wet vac to hold the water below the rim until we get there. We carry common pumps, so the swap usually happens on the spot. We will also figure out what killed the old pump.

    Wayne Sump Pump Swaps in Milford

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. Most dead Wayne pumps we see have a cooked motor or a float tangled in the power cord. We replace it with the right Wayne or another cast iron unit. Figure on a couple of hours.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Milford, DE

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Milford, DE

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. The new column gets set plumb and braced, and we set the float rod stops so the pump turns on and off at the right water levels. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Milford

    Coastal storms, nor'easters, and the occasional hurricane can flood low-lying parts of Delaware and overwhelm drainage and sewer systems. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. A high-water alarm lets you know the backup has taken over.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Milford, DE

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. We break out the concrete, set a larger poly basin with a sealed lid, and pour the floor back. A bigger basin means longer pump cycles. Fewer starts mean the motor lasts longer.

    Nearby Sump Pump Replacement in Milford, DE

    Groundwater levels shift with the seasons, and in a wet spring a pit can take on water every few minutes even with no rain in the forecast. We size the pump for that worst week. Not for an average day.

    Speed counts on a sump call. When a pump fails mid-storm, a basement can pick up inches of water fast, which is why the nearest tech gets the call. Most arrive with the right pump.
