    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Milford

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and typical code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater once a year is recommended in most homes. Near the coast around Milford, some shallow wells pick up a salty taste, which can corrode pipes and water heaters faster. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from impacting heating elements.

    Manufacturer tank warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a licensed plumber.

    Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are typical signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.
