    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Milford

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Milford, DE

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. A line that holds pressure at rest can still sag under full load. We test both.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Milford

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Skip that and the new unit wears out first. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Milford

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. We match the new unit to your gas line, vent type and mounting spot whenever we can. Most swaps take four to six hours.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Milford, DE

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Milford, DE

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. Both are in our wheelhouse.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Milford, DE

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. We handle that conversion. Gas and water connections usually line up.

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Shut the gas and cold isolation valve now. We then find out whether it is a fitting or the heat exchanger, since one is cheap and the other is replacement.
