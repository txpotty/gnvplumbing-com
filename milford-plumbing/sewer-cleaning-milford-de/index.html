    Jetting a Sewer Line in Milford, DE

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Milford

    Household sewers clog slowly. It takes months of buildup to stop a main. Each flush adds a little more to the pile at a rough joint or a low spot, until the 4 inch line is down to a trickle and the lowest drain in the house starts to back up, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Milford, DE

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. Guessing costs money. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Milford, DE

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Some homes from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s contain polybutylene pipe, which is best replaced before it fails. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Because a heavy cutter can punch through weak iron, we use chain descalers and a lower-pressure jet, then check the wall thickness on camera.

    Tree Root Removal in Milford, DE Sewers

    Roots get in through tiny gaps. Then they fill the pipe. We cut the root ball with a saw head sized for the pipe, then flush the leftovers with a jetter when the growth is heavy, and a camera shows where they are getting in.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Milford

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.
