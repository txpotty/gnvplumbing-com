    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Milford, DE

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Private wells are common in rural Delaware, and they can carry iron, nitrates, or low pH that calls for testing and treatment. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.
