    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Dover

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico runs them without electricity, using water flow to drive the valve. The price is higher.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Dover, DE

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Delaware homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Current Fleck valves fit standard tanks.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Dover

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. Salt use usually drops. We set the brine refill to your hardness so the system is not dumping salt down the drain every few days.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Dover, DE

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Dover, DE

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. Why soften water for the lawn?

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Dover, DE

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Sodium concerns are another reason.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Dover

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Softener Loop Installation in Dover, DE

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.
