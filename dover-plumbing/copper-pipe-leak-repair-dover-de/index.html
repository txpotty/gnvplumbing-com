    Pinhole Leak Repair in Dover

    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Dover

    Copper often runs under concrete slabs in Dover homes. Settling soil stresses it.     Split Copper Line Repair in Dover, DE

    A copper line can split from a freeze, a settling foundation or a hit from a nail. Splits leak hard.     Commercial Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Dover

    Commercial buildings often run larger diameter copper for domestic water. Water-off time hurts. We repair copper supply lines in offices, restaurants and apartment buildings, using press tools on large lines to shorten the shutdown and skip the torch. We schedule around business hours.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Maintenance in Dover, DE

    Copper gives warning signs before it leaks. They are easy to overlook. Green crust at fittings, a stain on a basement ceiling and high static pressure all point to trouble ahead, and a walkthrough of exposed copper in the basement, crawlspace or utility room catches many of them. A regulator often helps.

    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Dover

    Corrosion thins copper from the inside. Near the coast around Dover, some shallow wells pick up a salty taste, which can wear on pipes and water heaters faster. It shows up as green crust. On exposed pipe you may see blue-green deposits at fittings, and inside the wall the first sign is usually a pinhole or a weeping joint that stains the drywall below it. We replace the corroded length. Then we talk about the cause.
