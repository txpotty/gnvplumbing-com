    Picking a Tankless for Your Dover, DE House

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Dover, DE

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Hard freezes are usually short here, but a single cold night can still crack an outdoor faucet left connected to a hose. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Dover, DE

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Navien also makes combi units that heat the house and the water. We set up both.

    Navien Venting and Install in Dover

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. Our vent runs stay within spec. Warranty depends on it.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Dover

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. Sometimes a heat pump tank serves the house better, and we will say so.

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Dover, DE

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. A rack of four units can draw more than 600,000 BTU, so we confirm gas pressure at the far unit with every burner firing. We work after hours when needed.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Dover

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Close the cold isolation valve and the gas cock first. Then we look at whether the leak is a fitting or the heat exchanger itself, because one is a $20 fix and the other means a new unit.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Dover, DE

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Built-in pumps on Navien A and some Rinnai models push water through a crossover valve or a dedicated return. The pump runs on a timer. Usually just the morning and evening peaks.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Dover

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. The vent is plain PVC. The condensate is acidic, so we install a neutralizer cartridge and run the line to an approved drain. Humidity causes condensation on cold water lines and toilet tanks, which can slowly damage floors and encourage mold. The savings carry through every season because hot water use does.
