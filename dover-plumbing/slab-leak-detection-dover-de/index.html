    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Dover

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, finding where the sound is loudest and confirming it from another position, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.

    Listening Through Concrete in Dover

    The microphones pick up faint sounds. That means appliances, HVAC fans and traffic all show up in the headphones, so our techs shut off what they can and learn to tune out the rest, and that skill only comes with lots of practice. A pressurized line gives the clearest sound in your Dover home.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Dover

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, rubbing against concrete and gravel until it wears through. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Gas or electric bills may jump too.

    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Dover
