    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Dover, DE

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. The head comes off first. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Dover

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. Over time the cartridge or spool sticks, and you get a shower that will not stay at one temperature or a handle that turns past where it should before the water warms up. A new cartridge usually fixes it. If the valve is too old for parts, we talk through a replacement valve and what it means for the tile.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Dover

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. A new cartridge fixes it. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Dover

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. We fix the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Dover, DE

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Moen Posi-Temp showers take the 1222, and Delta Monitor 13 and 14 showers take the RP46074, and both wear out on a fairly regular clock. Both ride on our trucks. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Dover, DE

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Dover, DE

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not.     Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Dover, DE
