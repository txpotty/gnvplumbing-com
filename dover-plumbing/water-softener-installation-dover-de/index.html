    Water Softener Replacement in Dover, DE

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. We remove the old unit and install a new metered model, then check the loop and drain line while everything is apart. Metered valves save salt. Performance comes back right away.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Dover

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Then we program it. We set regeneration overnight.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Dover, DE

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. An existing loop keeps the price down, and adding a loop adds labor and materials. Carbon tanks cost extra. Your written quote lists equipment, labor and setup.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Dover, DE

    Building a home in Dover, DE? Adding a loop during framing is easy and cheap, since the plumber can route the main through the right spot before drywall goes up. Builders call us in early. We keep outdoor taps off the softener.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Dover, DE

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Dover, DE

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. Taste improves fast. We install twin-tank setups or dual-stage units.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Dover

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Dover

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. Bring your own unit. We set it up for you.
