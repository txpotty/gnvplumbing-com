    Water Softener Replacement in Dover, DE

    Older softeners can lose efficiency due to worn resin, failing valves or clogged injectors. We remove the old unit and install a new, more efficient model.     Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Dover

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for fully softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. GNV Plumbing carefully installs many models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Dover home.
