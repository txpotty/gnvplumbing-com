    To prevent future drain problems, avoid pouring grease down sinks, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles and be mindful of what goes down your toilets.     The duration of drain repairs depends entirely on the issue. A simple clog can often be cleared in an hour or two. More involved repairs, like replacing a section of a main sewer line, might take a full day or even several days, especially if permits or major digging are involved.
