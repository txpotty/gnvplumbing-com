    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Dover

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. Cracked china leaks eventually and heavy scale rarely comes clean, so a new bowl on the old carrier is usually best. Carrier bolts get inspected first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Dover, DE

    New urinals usually need a permit. Adding a fixture means new drain, vent and supply work that most cities inspect at rough-in and again at final, and commercial jobs often need plans that show fixture counts and accessible layouts. Permits are on us. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Dover

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Dover

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. Each unit goes on a carrier with new gaskets and stainless bolts, paired with a matched Sloan or Zurn valve. We check partition spacing too. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Dover

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Corroded hanger bolts and a flush valve seized to the spud mean we come prepared to cut, and we protect the wall tile while we do it so the new fixture does not sit against broken edges. We never reuse the old gasket. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Dover

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. We remove the flush valve, cap the supply and set the waterless bowl on the same carrier. Some codes want the supply left in place. That allows a future change back.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Dover, DE

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. A low-flow bowl and matching valve on the existing carrier cut water use sharply. Ask your water utility about rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Dover, DE
