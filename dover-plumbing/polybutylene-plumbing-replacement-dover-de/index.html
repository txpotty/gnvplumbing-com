    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Dover

    Polybutylene's inherent weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Private wells are common in rural Delaware, and they can carry iron, nitrates, or low pH that calls for testing and treatment. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already weakened.
