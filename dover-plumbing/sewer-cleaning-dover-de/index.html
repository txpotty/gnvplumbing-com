    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Dover

    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight. We clean these lines gently and use the camera to map joints and soft spots. If the pipe is failing, we will tell you plainly and show the footage. Cleaning keeps you running while you budget for a replacement.

    Tree Root Removal in Dover, DE Sewers

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line blockages, especially in Dover, DE neighborhoods where mature trees are prevalent. Roots seek out moisture and can work into even tiny cracks in sewer pipes, growing and eventually blocking the entire line. GNV Plumbing uses specialized root cutting tools attached to our drain augers and high-pressure hydro jetting to remove these invasive roots, restoring flow and preventing further damage. We can also advise on long-term solutions to protect your pipes.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Dover, DE

    A clogged main sewer line affects all drains in your home or business, often leading to multiple fixtures backing up at once. This severe issue requires prompt and expert attention. GNV Plumbing specializes in diagnosing and clearing main sewer line blockages in Dover, DE.     Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Dover

    For the most stubborn sewer line clogs, hydro jetting is the top choice. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to blast away grease, scale, mineral buildup, and even tree roots from the interior walls of your sewer pipes. GNV Plumbing employs professional hydro jetting equipment for sewer cleaning in Dover, delivering a thorough cleaning that leaves your pipes nearly spotless, greatly reducing the chance of future blockages and extending the life of your sewer system.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Dover, DE

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Some homes from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s contain polybutylene pipe, which is worth replacing before it fails. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a smart option.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Dover, DE

    Cleaning removes what is in the pipe, but it cannot fix a crack, a belly or a collapsed section. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera will often show why. We compare the yearly cost of maintenance cleanings to a spot repair, lining or replacement. Sometimes regular cleaning is the smart move for a few years. Other times a repair now saves more over time, and we show you the math.
