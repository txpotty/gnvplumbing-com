    Badger Disposal Install in Dover

    Most replacement disposals in local kitchens are a Badger. A 1/3 to 3/4 HP Badger handles normal scraps and fits almost any sink base cabinet. The unit hangs on its quick-lock mount, the discharge tube goes on, a cord kit gets added if needed and we finish with a cold-water test grind. About an hour, usually.

    New Construction Disposal Installs in Dover

    New builds and gut remodels get the disposal in two trips. At rough-in we set the drain height and confirm a switched outlet is in the sink base. The disposal itself waits. After the sink and faucet are set, we return to hang the unit, tie in the dishwasher and run the full water test.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Dover

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. We drive the plug out and fish it out of the chamber. The hose clamps onto the inlet and runs through an air gap or a high loop, which keeps dirty sink water from siphoning back into the tub. Both appliances get run together.

    Upfront Disposal Installation Pricing in Dover, DE

    Price depends mostly on two things: the unit you pick and how much needs to change under the sink. A like-for-like swap is the low end. First-time installs run higher, since a new flange and possibly a new outlet both add labor. We quote the job before any tools come out.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Dover

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. The unit hangs on the ring, locks with a quarter turn, and the discharge tube meets the trap on a new slip-joint washer. Power comes from a cord kit or the existing hardwire, checked for ground before the breaker goes back on.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Dover

    Kitchens that never had a disposal need some rework. First, the sink gets a disposal flange. The tailpiece and trap have to be reworked, and the cabinet needs a switched outlet, which means an electrician if there is no power under the sink already. Drain slope to the stack gets checked as well.

    Disposal Installation Cost Comparison in Dover, DE

    Cost differences come mostly from the unit and the prep work. A Badger 5 swap with a cord kit is at the bottom of the range. At the top is an Evolution Excel going into a kitchen that needs a new flange and a lower trap arm, with most jobs in between. You see horsepower and warranty length side by side.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Dover, DE

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. Expect the new unit to sit lower in the cabinet. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.
