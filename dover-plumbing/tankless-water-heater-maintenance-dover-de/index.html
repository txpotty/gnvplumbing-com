    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Dover

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means more stable temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Error Code Diagnostics in Dover, DE

    Codes like Rinnai LC, Navien E003 or Noritz 90 tell us a lot about what is going wrong. We read the code history, test flame rods, flow sensors and igniters and check gas pressure at the inlet. Many codes trace back to scale or a dirty filter and clear after maintenance. When a part has failed we explain the repair and the cost before doing any work.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Dover

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading correctly and the burner breathing properly.

    While some basic tasks like cleaning external filters might be possible for a homeowner, full maintenance, especially descaling the heat exchanger and inspecting gas lines, should be left to a licensed professional.     It is recommended to have your tankless water heater professionally serviced at least once a year. In areas with very hard water, or for heavy usage, more frequent maintenance, such as every six to nine months, might help to prevent scale buildup and maintain efficiency.

    Many manufacturers exclude scale damage from heat exchanger warranties. Regular, documented maintenance is the best way to keep your coverage intact.

    A standard tankless water heater maintenance service, including flushing and inspection, usually takes 1 to 2 hours. The exact time can vary depending on the unit's condition, the amount of scale buildup and any unforeseen issues encountered during the service.
