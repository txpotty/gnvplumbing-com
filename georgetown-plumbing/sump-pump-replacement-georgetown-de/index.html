    Which Sump Pump Fits Your Georgetown, DE Home

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Georgetown

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. Pumps and oil-sensing panels get replaced to code. Duplex setups with alternating pumps give you a spare if one quits. Insurers sometimes ask for proof.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Georgetown

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. New valve every time. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Georgetown, DE

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We swap in a corrosion-resistant pump and clear out leaves and silt. We route the outlet away from the house. Test them each spring.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Georgetown, DE

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They suit pits too tight for two separate pumps. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Georgetown

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Georgetown, DE

    Pit filling, pump not running? Call (302) 406-6354 and we will send someone. Keep the lid off, unplug anything sitting on the wet floor, and use a wet vac to hold the water below the rim until we get there. We carry common pumps, so the swap usually happens on the spot. We also check why the old one failed.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Georgetown

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. That is not a sump pump job. We replace the ejector and its discharge check valve, reseal the lid gasket, and confirm the vent ties in. Leaky lids let sewer gas into the basement.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Georgetown

    Horsepower matters less than people think. A third-horse pump moves plenty of water for most homes, as long as the vertical lift to the discharge is under about 15 feet. We check the flow chart against your actual lift before we pick a size. A pump that is too big empties the pit too fast and wears itself out.

    Local Sump Pump Replacement in Georgetown, DE

    Groundwater levels shift with the seasons, and in a wet spring a pit can take on water every few minutes even with no rain in the forecast. Our sizing is based on the wettest week of the year. Not for an average day.

    Local drainage matters. We look at the grading around your foundation and where the discharge line dumps, because water that comes right back toward the house keeps the pump running all night. Sometimes the fix is ten feet of pipe.
