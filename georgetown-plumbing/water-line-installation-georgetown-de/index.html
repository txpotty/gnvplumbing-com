    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Installation in Georgetown

    Most main service lines in Georgetown run $1,500 to $6,000. The price moves with the length, the pipe and the dig method, along with permits and how much lawn, concrete or planting has to be restored after the trench is closed and compacted. Trenchless costs more per foot.

    Installing a main is more than digging a trench. It takes code knowledge, a permit and connections at the meter, and a joint that is not made right can leak underground for months while the water bill climbs and the soil around your foundation soaks. A licensed plumber is the safe bet.

    In most communities the homeowner owns the line from the meter to the house, and the utility owns the meter and the street side. Your water provider confirms the split before we start.

    Parts and labor are both covered on our installs. The exact terms are written into your project agreement, and we keep a record of the pipe, fittings and test results so any future warranty claim goes quickly. Hold on to your copy.

    Uponor Wirsbo and Viega PureFlow cover most of our PEX work. Inside the house we run Type L copper, and buried runs get Type K, sourced from manufacturers that meet ASTM standards, so the pipe holds up in the conditions it will actually see. Wall thickness matters in the ground.

    Depth rules depend on your jurisdiction and climate. Shutting off and draining outdoor spigots in the fall and insulating crawlspace pipes does a lot toward preventing winter leaks. Lines have to sit below the local frost line, so colder places need deeper trenches than mild ones.

    For PEX we usually install Uponor, Viega or Zurn systems with fittings from the same maker. Copper comes from manufacturers that meet ASTM standards, and we pick the brand and pipe grade based on your run length, your soil and what your local code allows for direct burial in Georgetown. Mixing brands can void warranties.
