    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Georgetown, DE

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. On a slab, we cut concrete. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Georgetown

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. Linear drains help a lot. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.
