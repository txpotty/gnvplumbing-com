    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Georgetown

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Georgetown, DE

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and works well where exterior access is limited.

    Drainage System Integration in Georgetown, DE

    Effective walls waterproofing often requires a solid drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Georgetown, DE properties, so water is managed well and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Georgetown, DE

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to verify the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test proves the pan is watertight before tile.
