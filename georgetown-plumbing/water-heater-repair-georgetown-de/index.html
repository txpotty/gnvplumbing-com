    Pilot Light Repair in Georgetown

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Less often it is a dirty flame arrestor screen choking off combustion air. We test the thermocouple output in millivolts. A good reading points us to the gas valve instead.

    Gas Water Heater Repair in Georgetown, DE

    Gas units add a burner, a pilot or igniter and a gas control valve to the list of parts that can fail. Most no-heat calls trace to the thermocouple or the gas control. We leak test each gas connection after the repair. Draft at the vent gets checked too.

    Rheem, Bradford White, State and American Standard are the tank brands we see most, with Navien and Rinnai leading on tankless. Each brand has its own weak spots.     Tank Water Heater Repair in Georgetown

    Most homes still run a 50-gallon tank. Thermostats, elements, anodes and relief valves make up most tank repairs. A good flush takes care of a lot of noise and slow recovery problems on its own, no parts required.

    Navien, Rinnai, Noritz and Rheem tankless units show error codes on the front panel that help us narrow the problem quickly. Common causes are scale on the heat exchanger, a dirty inlet filter, a failed flow sensor or a weak igniter. Descaling uses a small pump and approved solution.     Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in Georgetown
