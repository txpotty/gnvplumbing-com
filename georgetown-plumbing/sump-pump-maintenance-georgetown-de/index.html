    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Georgetown, DE

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Georgetown, DE.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Georgetown, DE

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Georgetown

    Our team is experienced in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Georgetown due to their quiet operation and compact design. Maintenance involves pulling the pump to inspect the watertight seals, clearing the intake screen and checking the internal components for corrosion or wear. We also verify the electrical connections are secure and free from moisture, avoiding electrical hazards and supporting longevity.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Georgetown

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Georgetown, DE

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, GNV Plumbing provides dedicated maintenance services in Georgetown, DE. We focus on inspecting the external motor, ensuring proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and support reliable performance during heavy rains.
