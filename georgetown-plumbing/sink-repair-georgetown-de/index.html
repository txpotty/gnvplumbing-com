    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Georgetown, DE

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Georgetown

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Hard freezes are usually short here, but a single cold night can still crack an outdoor faucet left connected to a hose. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. Hose bib leaks are part of the same call. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Georgetown, DE

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. Either the clevis slipped on the lift rod or hair has wrapped the stopper so it will not seat. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Georgetown, DE

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Georgetown

    Check the slip nuts first. Plastic slip nuts crack when they are overtightened with pliers, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until water seeps out at every joint along the trap. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Georgetown, DE

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Georgetown, DE

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Georgetown

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Then lay towels down. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.
