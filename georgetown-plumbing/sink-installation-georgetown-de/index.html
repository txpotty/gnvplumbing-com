    How We Set New Sinks in Georgetown, DE

    Vessel Sink Installation in Georgetown, DE

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Georgetown, DE

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. The faucet holes have to match the faucet you picked, whether that is a single hole, a 4 inch centerset or an 8 inch widespread, and the overflow has to line up with the pop-up drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Faucet and Disposal Hookup in Georgetown, DE

    New sinks often get a new disposal. We mount the disposal on a fresh flange with putty, connect the dishwasher knockout so the hose drains through it, and confirm the outlet sits above the trap inlet so it drains properly. Forgetting the knockout plug floods the dishwasher. We run both before leaving.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Georgetown, DE

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Georgetown, DE

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. The backsplash gets sealed last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Commercial Restroom Sink Installation in Georgetown

    Public restroom lavatories take a beating. We install wall-hung and countertop units on carriers, add sensor or metering faucets, and set thermostatic mixing valves where code requires tempered water at public hand sinks. Vandal-resistant parts are available. Spec sheets go to your general contractor.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Georgetown

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. The basin itself hangs on a bracket lagged into 2x blocking behind the drywall, and if the old wall has no blocking we open it, add a board between the studs, and patch it back. Drywall anchors do not cut it. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Georgetown

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. The bowl stays braced until our return. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.
