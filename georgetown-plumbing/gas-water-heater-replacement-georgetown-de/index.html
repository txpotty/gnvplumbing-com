    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Georgetown, DE House

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Georgetown, DE

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Georgetown

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. We disconnect the old unit and reconnect the new one to the existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. A tank in a tight closet needs louvered doors or a ducted air supply, and we check that before lighting it.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Georgetown, DE

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Georgetown

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. A dedicated outlet gets confirmed first.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Georgetown, DE

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. You will need a condensate drain and a nearby outlet. Payback depends on usage.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Georgetown

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. A pressure regulator or backflow preventer on the main stops heated water from pushing back to the street, so pressure climbs every time the burner runs. Code commonly calls for an expansion tank. We set its air charge to match house pressure.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Georgetown

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Delaware homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We measure the closet before ordering. Most of these swaps take two to three hours.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Georgetown, DE

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Commercial code is tighter. Sizing comes from peak demand and recovery rate.
