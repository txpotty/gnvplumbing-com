    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Georgetown

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be damaging and difficult to detect. These leaks often stem from corroded or damaged pipes embedded within or under your home's slab. GNV Plumbing uses advanced leak detection technology to locate the exact source, then expertly repairs or reroutes the compromised pipe, often using durable PEX to bypass the slab entirely, avoiding future issues in the same spot.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Georgetown, DE

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you compare the cost of both paths.

    Whole-Home Repiping in Georgetown

    If your home in Georgetown is older and experiencing frequent leaks, low water pressure throughout, or discolored water from multiple fixtures, a whole-home repiping might be the most cost-effective long-term solution. This upgrades your entire water delivery system, greatly improving water quality and pressure while eliminating future leak concerns from aging infrastructure.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Georgetown, DE

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace reachable sections with PVC or ABS. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage in hand.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Georgetown, DE

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a common choice for modern pipe replacement projects in Georgetown due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion and freezing.
