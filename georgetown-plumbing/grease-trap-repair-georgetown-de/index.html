    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Georgetown, DE

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. Pretreatment programs commonly set the limit at 25 percent of the liquid depth for grease and solids combined.     Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Georgetown, DE

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. The inlet tee sends flow down below the grease layer. New tees go in as Schedule 40 PVC, cut to the drop length the manufacturer or local code specifies.
