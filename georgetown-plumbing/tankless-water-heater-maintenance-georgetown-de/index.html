    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Georgetown, DE

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Georgetown, DE

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Georgetown, DE

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. You get a written report with the readings, so next year's visit has something to compare against.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Georgetown

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. Gas connections get a leak test, and we work the pressure relief valve. The air filter comes out and the igniter gets a look. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Georgetown

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. They see heavy use, and scale builds faster than at home. We descale each heater in the bank, check the cascade controller and confirm the load is shared evenly across every unit. Quarterly service is common.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Georgetown

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Georgetown

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. Once the cycle ends, we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Georgetown, DE

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Georgetown, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. We follow the manufacturer's steps, not a generic checklist. That matters for warranty claims down the road.
