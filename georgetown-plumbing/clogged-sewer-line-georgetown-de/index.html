    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Georgetown

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That matters a lot. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale while the nozzle drives itself forward. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it.     Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Georgetown, DE

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Not even close. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they form a dense plug that a cable can poke a hole through but not fully remove from the pipe. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Toss wipes in the trash.

    Emergency Sewer Line Clog Clearing in Georgetown, DE

    A fully blocked sewer will push raw sewage out the lowest drain in the building. Often the basement or a shower. GNV Plumbing sends an emergency tech across Georgetown, DE with the full kit, opens the line and keeps the mess contained. The spill area gets cleaned.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Georgetown, DE

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. It scrubs the whole wall. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, while a jetter removes the grease, scale and fine roots coating the pipe so the next clog has nothing to start on. The camera goes in before the jetter. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Georgetown

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Often all three together. Grease coats the pipe, wipes catch on it and roots lock everything into a plug. We clear it with a sewer machine, then camera the line to see which one started it.

    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Georgetown

    Clay and older cast iron sewer pipe have a joint every few feet. Each joint is an opening. Roots from trees and shrubs push into the gaps and fill the pipe, so we cut them with a sewer machine fitted with root saws and then run a camera to see how much damage remains. Ongoing root problems may call for a treatment schedule or lining.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Georgetown, DE

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Cracked pipe is different. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, so the camera footage decides whether you spend a few hundred dollars on cleaning or more on a spot fix, liner or replacement. We walk you through it.
