    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Georgetown

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent.     Pilot Light Repair in Georgetown

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.
