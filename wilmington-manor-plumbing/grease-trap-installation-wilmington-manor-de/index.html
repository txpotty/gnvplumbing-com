    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Wilmington Manor

    Small indoor hydromechanical traps typically cost $1,500 to $5,000 installed. Large outdoor gravity interceptors often run $8,000 to $30,000 or more with excavation, concrete and site restoration. We provide a written estimate after a site visit.

    Determining the correct grease trap size involves calculating your kitchen's wastewater flow rate, fixture count, and peak operating hours. Our experienced plumbers will conduct a complete assessment of your Wilmington Manor business to recommend the appropriate size and type of grease trap that meets local codes and your operational needs.

    While the terms are often used interchangeably, 'grease trap' typically refers to smaller, indoor hydro-mechanical units, often found under sinks. 'Grease interceptor' usually refers to larger, outdoor gravity-fed units with higher capacities. Both serve the same purpose of separating FOG from wastewater.

    The primary purpose of a grease trap is to intercept fats, oils, and greases (FOG) from commercial kitchen wastewater before they enter the public sewer system. This prevents pipe blockages, protects municipal infrastructure, and helps businesses comply with environmental regulations.

    GNV Plumbing provides a warranty on our workmanship for all grease trap installations. Additionally, the grease trap unit itself will typically come with a manufacturer's warranty. We will provide you with all warranty details at the time of installation, giving you peace of mind.

    We calculate flow from every connected fixture, including sinks, floor sinks and dish machines. Hydromechanical units are usually rated by gallons per minute, while gravity interceptors are sized by volume and retention time.

    Grease trap installation is a complex commercial plumbing task that requires specialized knowledge, tools, and adherence to strict local codes.     The lifespan of a grease trap depends on its type, material, and how well it's maintained. Steel grease traps may last 10 to 15 years, while durable fiberglass or polyethylene units can last 20 to 30 years or even longer. Regular cleaning and proper usage are key to extending its lifespan.
