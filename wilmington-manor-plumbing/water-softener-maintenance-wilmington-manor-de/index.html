    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in Wilmington Manor, DE

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. The top layer fuses into a crust with an empty pocket below it. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, which leaves the resin without a recharge and hard water flows to every tap.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Wilmington Manor, DE

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold. We pull the injector and screen, clean them, and replace the seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1 if they are worn.

    Commercial Twin-Tank Maintenance in Wilmington Manor

    Salons, car washes, hotels and restaurants put far more gallons through a softener than any house does, and a hard-water day can mean spotted glassware or a scaled ice machine. On twin-tank and progressive-flow units, we spend extra time on the meter and the tank switching. We service them every one to three months, depending on volume.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Wilmington Manor

    Resin does the actual softening. The beads swap sodium for calcium and magnesium, and once they are loaded up they need brine to recharge. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to tell whether the bed is still at full strength.
