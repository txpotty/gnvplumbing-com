    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Wilmington Manor, DE

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. We can check whether yours qualifies. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard use their own cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Wilmington Manor, DE

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. If the handle leaks, snugging the adjusting ring under the cap often fixes it.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Wilmington Manor

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. We replace hoses, spray heads and weights with brand parts, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Wilmington Manor

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. A flush of the lines usually fixes it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Wilmington Manor

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Freezing weather arrives most winters in Delaware, and pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls, and unheated garages are the most likely to freeze. Packing and seat washers get replaced on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. Every new spigot gets a vacuum breaker for backflow protection.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Wilmington Manor

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. A new faucet can usually go in on the same visit, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Wilmington Manor

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. Anything worn gets replaced, and the rest gets checked. Each valve type, from compression to ceramic disc, wears in its own way, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Wilmington Manor

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. First we identify which type of valve you have. It matters. We carry the common washers, seats and cartridges, so the fix usually happens on the first trip.
