    Type L Copper Repiping in Wilmington Manor

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. We use Type L, not the thinner Type M, and we support it with the right hangers so it does not rub or rattle in the walls. Lead-free solder only. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Wilmington Manor, DE

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Wilmington Manor

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. Our crews use Uponor or similar PEX-A, where the pipe end gets expanded and shrinks back over the fitting for a full-diameter connection. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Wilmington Manor

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Wilmington Manor

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it. First we stop the leak and get water back on with a temporary fix, then we schedule the repipe without pressure. That gives you time. Your policy may pay for water damage even if not for the new pipe, so we take photos of everything we find.

    PEX Repiping in Wilmington Manor, DE

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Wilmington Manor, DE

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.
