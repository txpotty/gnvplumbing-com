    Pilot Light Repair in Wilmington Manor

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Wilmington Manor

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and trip the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as needed.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Wilmington Manor, DE

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to uneven water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water. Our team can test these key components for proper operation, checking for electrical continuity and gas flow. If a part is defective, we replace it with a quality, manufacturer-approved component so your water heater heats evenly and safely.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Wilmington Manor, DE

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Wilmington Manor

    A worn burner assembly can result in insufficient heating or unusual noises. We carefully inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear. We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is essential for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Wilmington Manor home. Proper venting is a critical safety consideration.
