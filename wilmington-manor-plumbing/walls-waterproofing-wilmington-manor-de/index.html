    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Wilmington Manor, DE

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Wilmington Manor, DE

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Rapid growth in coastal and suburban areas means many newer homes in Delaware are built with PEX supply lines and PVC drains. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. A rigid coating on soft brick traps moisture and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile. We repoint failed mortar joints before sealing.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Wilmington Manor

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Wilmington Manor

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Wilmington Manor, DE

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation.     Shower Wall Waterproofing in Wilmington Manor

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Most systems we install should go 20 to 30 years or longer. Life span comes down to how it was installed, what method was used and what the soil around the house is like. The sump pump will need replacing well before that. Plan on a new pump every seven to ten years.

    Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard and Laticrete Hydro Ban cover most of our showers. Sheet membranes like Kerdi suit tight schedules because there is nothing to cure, while liquids like RedGard and Hydro Ban go on in two coats and need to dry between them.     Bubbling paint is a big one.     Interior systems manage water after it has come through the wall, with drain tile along the footing and a sump to pump it out.
