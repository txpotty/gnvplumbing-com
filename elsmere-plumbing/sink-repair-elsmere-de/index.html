    Common Sink Repairs in Elsmere, DE

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Elsmere, DE

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. Most single-faucet pressure problems are a packed aerator screen, and after that we look at the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Low pressure everywhere points to a different problem. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Elsmere, DE

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Elsmere

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. We reset the bowl on new clips. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Elsmere, DE

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. A trap or stopper costs a fraction of a new sink, and replacement only makes sense when the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain body has rusted into the basin. We quote both ways if it is a close call. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Elsmere, DE

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Hair Clog Clearing in Elsmere

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. First the stopper comes out and gets cleaned, then a hand auger goes through the trap and wall arm, with no chemicals. We flush the overflow channel while we are there. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Elsmere

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink. A down hand sink can cost you an inspection, so we work around your hours and carry commercial faucets and basket strainers. Most commercial clogs are grease. We also check the grease interceptor connection when the drain backs up.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Elsmere

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.
