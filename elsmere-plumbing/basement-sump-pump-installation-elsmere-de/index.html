    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Elsmere

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Elsmere, DE

    Power outages are common, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. We offer and install battery backup sump pump systems that automatically activate if the power goes out, providing continuous protection for your basement in Elsmere, DE.     Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Elsmere

    Office buildings, parking structures and multi-unit properties with below-grade levels usually need duplex sump systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. The panel switches lead and lag pumps on each cycle so wear stays even, and a high water alarm alerts building staff.
