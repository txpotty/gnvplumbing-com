    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Pike Creek Valley

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls.     Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Pike Creek Valley, DE

    The flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube and body are where leaks start. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed.     Preventive Care and Longevity in Pike Creek Valley, DE

    Keep cold water running before, during and after use, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Celery, artichoke leaves and corn husks belong in the trash.
