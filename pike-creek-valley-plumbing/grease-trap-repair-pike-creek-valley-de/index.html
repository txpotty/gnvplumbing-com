    Damaged Baffle Repair in Pike Creek Valley

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.
