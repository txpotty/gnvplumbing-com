    In many cases, a plumbing permit is required for new urinal installations or significant plumbing modifications in Pike Creek Valley. GNV Plumbing can help you understand the specific permit requirements for your project and ensure all work complies with local building codes.

    Flush urinals use a water flush to remove waste, while waterless urinals use a specialized trap system with a sealant liquid to prevent odors without water.     Regular cleaning with appropriate restroom cleaners is essential. For waterless urinals, follow manufacturer guidelines for cartridge replacement. For flushed urinals, ensure the flush mechanism is working correctly and address any slow draining issues promptly to prevent blockages.
