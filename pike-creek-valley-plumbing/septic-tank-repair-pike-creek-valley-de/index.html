    Drain Field Line Jetting in Pike Creek Valley

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping.     Effluent Pump Replacement in Pike Creek Valley, DE

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor. Our tech pulls the old unit and checks the discharge before the new one goes in. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Pike Creek Valley, DE

    Some tanks have no riser, so we look at the baffles through the small inspection port with a push camera. That saves digging up the whole lid. If the outlet tee is cracked or missing, we dig at that end only, then install a PVC tee with a filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.

    Septic Tee Repair in Pike Creek Valley

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer flows straight into the field lines and seals the trenches. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Leach Field Restoration in Pike Creek Valley, DE

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A tilted or crushed D-box can send everything to one trench and drown it. A new or leveled box brings a lot of fields back. Some fields are past saving. For those, we explain the design and permit steps.
