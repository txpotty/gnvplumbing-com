    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Newark, DE

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there.     A spot repair or a jetting job usually takes a few hours. Lining takes a day or two. A full replacement with excavation can take two to five days, depending on the length of the run, the depth of the pipe and how long the inspection takes to schedule. The schedule is in the written estimate.
