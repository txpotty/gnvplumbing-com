    Lasting Sewer Repair Services in Newark, DE

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Newark

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to repeat clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Newark, DE

    Tree and shrub roots go right for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than ongoing cleaning.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Newark, DE

    Undetected sewer line leaks can lead to unpleasant odors, wet spots in your yard and even structural damage to your home's foundation. GNV Plumbing uses accurate leak detection methods to pinpoint the source of the leak, whether it's a minor crack or a significant break. Once identified, we provide targeted repairs, from patching small leaks to replacing compromised sections of pipe, ensuring a watertight system.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Newark

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can often use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Newark, DE

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Older homes in the north often have basements, while many newer and coastal homes sit on slabs or elevated foundations. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Newark, DE

    When repairs are no longer enough due to extensive damage, age or material failure, sewer pipe replacement becomes necessary. We offer several replacement options, including traditional trenching and modern trenchless technologies like pipe bursting or pipe lining. Our experts will assess your situation and recommend the most cost-effective and least disruptive option for your Delaware property, ensuring a long-lasting new sewer line.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Newark

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact location and depth of every problem on the surface. You get a copy of the video and a plain-language summary of what we found. This step alone frequently saves homeowners from paying for repairs they do not need.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Newark, DE

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Newark.

    Trenchless sewer repair is a method that allows for pipe repair or replacement without major digging. It's an excellent option for preserving your property while fixing underground pipes.

    Trenchless sewer repair is a method of repairing or replacing sewer lines with minimal digging, often requiring only one or two access points. It involves techniques like pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, or pipe lining, where a resin-impregnated liner is inserted and cured inside the existing pipe. This preserves your landscaping and is less disruptive than traditional methods.
