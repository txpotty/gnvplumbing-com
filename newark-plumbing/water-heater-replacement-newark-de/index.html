    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Newark

    Electric water heaters in Newark usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in Newark

    Bradford White sells only through the trade, so you will not find one at a big-box store. Their tanks have a good reputation for durability, and parts and warranty support run through the plumber who installed it, which makes it simple for us to handle a warranty claim for a homeowner down the road. Supply houses stock them.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Newark

    Businesses in Newark run their water heaters harder than homes do, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons, laundromats and offices each have a different demand curve, and we size the new unit by peak gallons per hour and the temperature health code requires, which can mean a larger tank, a booster or a rack of tankless units. We schedule around your hours.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Newark

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. We price both options.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Newark, DE

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Newark, DE homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Newark

    Rapid growth in coastal and suburban areas means many newer homes in Delaware are built with PEX supply lines and plastic drains. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. Two-person crew, always.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Newark

    We use Bradford White a lot on our trucks because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. When replacing one, we match fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty still applies, and register the new heater right away. We file the warranty.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Newark, DE

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.
