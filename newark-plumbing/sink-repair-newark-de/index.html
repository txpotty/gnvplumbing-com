    Leaking P-Trap Repair in Newark

    Most puddles under a bathroom sink start at the P-trap. Slip-joint washers flatten over time, plastic traps crack, and old chrome traps rust through from the inside. We replace the trap with a correctly sized 1-1/4 inch assembly, align the tailpiece and wall arm, and run water to confirm a dry seal. This is one of the quickest and least expensive fixes we do.

    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in Newark, DE

    When the stopper will not close, will not open or lets water slowly escape, the pop-up linkage is usually to blame. The pivot rod nut can leak, the clevis strap can slip, and hair wraps around the stopper body. We clean or replace the assembly and adjust it so the lift rod works freely. If the drain body itself is corroded, we replace it with a matching finish.
