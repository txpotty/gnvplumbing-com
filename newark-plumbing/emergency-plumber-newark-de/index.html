    Common Plumbing Emergencies in Newark, DE

    Leaking Tank Water Heaters in Newark, DE

    Sediment from hard water shortens tank life, and the first leak often shows up at the bottom seam. A relief valve that drips after a hot shower is a separate problem, often high pressure or a failed expansion tank. We repair valves, thermocouples and connections, and when a tank has rusted through we can usually swap it the same day. Rheem and AO Smith are the brands we see most.

    Frozen Pipe Thawing and Repair in Newark, DE

    Shutting off and draining outdoor spigots in the fall and insulating crawlspace pipes goes a long way toward preventing winter leaks. Start by opening the faucet on that line. We warm frozen pipe slowly with heat guns and pipe heaters, never a torch, working from the faucet end back toward the ice so pressure has somewhere to go. If the line already split, we cut out the damaged length and replace it, then suggest insulation or heat cable for that run.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Newark, DE

    The sound of running water with everything off is a textbook slab leak symptom. So is a hot spot on the floor. We isolate the hot or cold side first, so you keep partial water overnight, then locate the leak with acoustic and thermal gear. Repairs include a direct cut through the slab, a reroute through the walls or attic, or a partial repipe of that branch.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Newark

    A basement pump that quits halfway through a heavy rain may let inches of water into the basement in an hour. Common causes are a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor or a check valve that let go. Our plumber tests the switch and motor and swaps the pump if needed. Then we trace the discharge line outside. If the discharge line is crushed or frozen, water just circles back to the pit.

    Sewer Line Backups in Newark, DE

    Sewage coming up a floor drain or the lowest tub in the house is a main line blockage until proven otherwise. Stop running water. We open the outside cleanout, clear the line with a cable machine, then run a camera to see whether roots, grease or a broken joint caused it. We show you the video.

    Water Heater Emergencies in Newark

    Water pooling under a tank usually means the inner liner has rusted through, which no repair can fix. A leak at the top is different. It is often a loose union, a failed flex connector or a weeping relief valve, and those are repairs we can finish the same night on Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith and most other brands. If the tank is done, we shut it down safely and set up a quick replacement.

    Severe Drain Clog Clearing in Newark, DE

    Grease, wipes and scale are behind the bulk of our emergency drain calls. Dishwasher backups are usually branch clogs. Water rising in the tub when the toilet flushes points to the main line, which calls for a cable machine run from the cleanout. We open it, flush it, then run a camera if it clogged before.

    Overflowing Toilet Help in Newark, DE

    Turn the oval valve behind the toilet clockwise. The tank stops refilling once that valve closes. We clear the blockage with a closet auger, which will not scratch porcelain, and check whether the clog sits in the trap or further down the line, since a toilet that overflows every week usually means a main line problem. A failed fill valve or a seized stop gets replaced on the same visit.
