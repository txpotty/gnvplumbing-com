    If your home has a pressure regulator or backflow device, heated water has nowhere to expand. An expansion tank absorbs that pressure and protects the heater and fixtures.

    Most cities and counties in Delaware require a permit for water heater replacement because it involves gas and venting. We handle the permit when one is required.

    Atmospheric vent water heaters use natural convection to vent combustion gases up a chimney or flue. Power vent models use an electric fan to force gases out, allowing for more flexible venting options and often higher efficiency due to cleaner combustion.

    Most gas tanks last 8 to 12 years. Sediment from hard water and extreme garage temperatures can shorten that. Annual flushing and anode checks help them last for more years.
