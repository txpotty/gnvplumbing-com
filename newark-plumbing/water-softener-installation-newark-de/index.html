    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Newark

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. Bring your own unit. We set it up for you.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Newark, DE

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Newark

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Newark

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. Our residential installs start with a test and a few questions about water use, then we pick a grain capacity that regenerates about once a week for your household. Weekly is the sweet spot. Every detail is handled on one visit.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Newark, DE

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Newark

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. Sizing comes first. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Newark

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron adds to the setting. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Newark, DE

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.
