    Ball Faucet Repair in Newark, DE

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's probably time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are typically the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Newark

    That incessant drip, drip, drip isn't just annoying, it's a sign of a deeper problem. Most dripping faucets are caused by internal component wear, like a deteriorated rubber washer or a cracked cartridge. Our team provides precise solutions, replacing the specific parts that are failing. We work with all types of faucets, including compression, cartridge, disc, and ball varieties, restoring a quiet and drip-free operation once again.

    Delta & Moen Faucet Repair in Newark, DE

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Newark

    Weak or sputtering flow is usually caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-home water pressure.

    Kitchen Faucet Repair in Newark

    Kitchen faucets endure heavy daily use and are prone to specific issues like sprayer malfunctions, clogged aerators, or swivel problems.     Compression Faucet Repair in Newark

    Compression faucets, often found in older Newark homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's typically due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks for good. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Newark

We repair leaking packing nuts, replace worn washers and install new frost-free hose bibs when needed. We also add vacuum breakers to meet backflow prevention requirements. A leaking hose bib can waste a lot of water over a long season of yard use.
