    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Newark

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Many need no power. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    Gas Line Upgrades for Water Heaters in Newark, DE

    Moving up to a bigger or tankless gas heater can outgrow the existing gas line.     Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Newark

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. Each install gets a new gas connector and drip leg, a vent connector pitched upward and a relief line piped where code allows. Strapping where required. Your household sets the size.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Newark

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. We inspect the existing vent. We replace bad sections.
