    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Newark

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet typically costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    Slow drains in several fixtures, gurgling toilets and sewage odors are typical signs. Water backing up into a tub when you flush is another clue. Frequent clogs in the same line also suggest a deeper blockage.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a lasting fix.

    No permit is needed for routine rooting. Installing a new cleanout or repairing pipe may require one. We handle permits for any repair work.

    Homes with tree roots or older clay pipe often need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Homes with newer PVC lines may go many years without it. A camera inspection can help set the right schedule.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and throw away fibrous materials like coffee grounds and wipes in the trash. Consider periodic professional drain maintenance or hydro-jetting, especially for older homes or those with a history of recurring blockages.

    Our technicians take every precaution to keep your home clean during rooter service. We use drop cloths and protective gear to contain any mess, so the work area is tidy after the job is done. We respect your property and aim for a clean, efficient service.

    While chemical drain cleaners are available, GNV Plumbing generally advises against them. They often only partially clear clogs, can damage pipes over time, and pose safety risks. Professional rooter services are much more effective, safer for your plumbing, and provide a lasting solution to blockages.
