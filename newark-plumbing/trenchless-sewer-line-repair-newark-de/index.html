    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Newark

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Solutions in Newark

Using trusted brands like Perma-Liner supports the integrity and longevity of our work.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Newark, DE

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Delaware homeowners.

    Pipe Bursting in Newark, DE

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Newark

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Trenchless repair is highly versatile and effective for issues like cracks, fractures, root intrusion and minor collapses.     A properly installed liner has no joints for roots to enter, so root intrusion is essentially eliminated in the lined section.
