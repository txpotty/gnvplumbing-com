    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Newark, DE

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are equipped to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can show up as wet spots or high water bills in your Newark, DE home. We have separate methods for each.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Newark, DE

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Newark

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.

    Commercial Slab Leak Detection in Newark, DE

    Commercial properties in Newark, DE also face the risk of slab leaks, which can disrupt business operations and lead to serious structural damage. GNV Plumbing offers dedicated detection services for businesses, using the same technology to minimize downtime. We work quickly to locate leaks in offices, retail spaces and other commercial buildings.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Newark, DE

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area quickly without any physical disruption to your property in Newark.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Newark, DE

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also look like slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is vital if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Newark home.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Newark, DE

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as possible.
