    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Newark, DE

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is vital for proper diagnosis and repair in Newark, DE. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are usually isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at finding the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.

    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Newark, DE

    Much of Delaware sits on sandy coastal plain soil with a high water table, which affects slab, crawlspace, and septic design. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. We can then quote a spot repair to restore correct slope.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Newark

    GNV Plumbing uses powerful hydro-jetting technology to clear even the most stubborn sewer line blockages. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away grease, scale, mineral buildup, and tree roots, restoring your pipes to their original diameter. It's a very effective, non-invasive way to clean your sewer lines thoroughly and prevent future clogs. Unlike snaking, hydro-jetting cleans the whole pipe wall.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Newark

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another backup.

    Cooking grease cools and hardens in the main line, and so-called flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does.
