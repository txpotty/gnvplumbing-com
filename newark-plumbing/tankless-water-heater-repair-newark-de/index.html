    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, restricting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models.     Ignition Failure Repair in Newark, DE

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet.     Recirculation Pump Repair in Newark

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water faster. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water. We test the pump, check valves and controls and set the schedule to match your routine. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Newark, DE

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.
