    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Newark

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go.     Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Newark, DE

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Newark

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Newark, DE

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Batteries need replacing every few years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Newark, DE

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. Finished basements are the usual reason. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Newark

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Newark, DE

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. We list each option on a written quote.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Newark
