    How We Set New Sinks in Wilmington, DE

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Wilmington, DE

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Wilmington, DE

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Wilmington, DE

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. Three-compartment sinks usually drain indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water within easy reach of every prep station on the line. We read the plan review first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Wilmington

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Strainers are often fused on, the disposal has to come off, and the dishwasher hose needs a fresh high loop. Then the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Wilmington, DE

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Clips pull it tight to the counter. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Sink Installation Cost Factors in Wilmington, DE
