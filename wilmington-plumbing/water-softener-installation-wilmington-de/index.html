    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Wilmington, DE

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. GNV Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Wilmington

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. The savings add up. Several models are available.

    Water Softener Replacement in Wilmington, DE

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. You will notice soft water the same day.
