    Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in Wilmington

    Sprinkler supply lines sit shallow and break easily. Shovels and roots get them. A broken irrigation main can run up a water bill fast in the growing season, and we fix the main, the backflow connection and the feed to the valves. We can reroute lines away from trees.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Wilmington, DE

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.
