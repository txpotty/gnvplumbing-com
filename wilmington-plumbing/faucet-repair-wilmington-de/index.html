    Disc Faucet Repair in Wilmington

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Wilmington

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. It happens faster with hard water. The valve body gets cleaned while the old part is out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Wilmington, DE

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. Each brand uses its own part number. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Wilmington

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Shutting off and draining outdoor spigots in the fall and insulating crawlspace pipes goes a long way toward preventing winter leaks. Packing and seat washers get replaced on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. A vacuum breaker goes on every new one.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Wilmington

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. Flushing usually clears it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Wilmington, DE

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. We can check whether yours qualifies. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard each use proprietary cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Wilmington, DE

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. A seats and springs kit fixes most of them. Loose handles happen too. If the handle leaks, snugging the adjusting ring under the cap often fixes it.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Wilmington

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. We replace hoses, spray heads and weights with brand parts, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.
