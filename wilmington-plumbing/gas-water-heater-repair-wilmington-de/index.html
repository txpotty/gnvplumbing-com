    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Wilmington

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Wilmington, DE can vary a lot depending on the issue.     Many Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves use flash codes to report problems like a weak thermopile or a tripped sensor. We read the code and test the related part.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't detect heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a very common reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a vital safety component.

    Most repairs are finished the same day in one to two hours. Special order gas valves may take one to three business days.

    In most cases, minor repairs like replacing a thermocouple or a thermostat do not require a permit. We handle all required permitting.

    Typical causes include a weak thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube, a clogged flame arrestor or poor combustion air. Drafts in the garage can also snuff out older pilots.
