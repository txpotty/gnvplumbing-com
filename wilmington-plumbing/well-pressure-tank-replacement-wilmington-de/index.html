    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Wilmington, DE

    A constant pressure system pairs a variable speed drive like the Franklin SubDrive or Grundfos SQE with a smaller pressure tank. The pump speeds up or slows down to hold a set pressure, so showers do not fade when a sprinkler kicks on. These systems cost more, often $2,500 to $5,000, but they eliminate pressure swings. They also reduce hard starts on the motor. We explain whether your well and pump are good candidates.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Wilmington, DE

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop, typically at 30/50 or 40/60 psi. Pitted contacts, insect nests and a clogged sensing nipple are frequent causes of failure in well houses. We replace worn switches with Square D or similar models and clean or replace the nipple below it. A low-pressure cutoff switch can also be added to stop the pump if the well runs dry. We set the switch to match your new tank's pre-charge.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Wilmington

    The cost for well pressure tank replacement in Wilmington, DE typically ranges from $800 to $2,000. This depends on the tank size, brand, system complexity, and any additional components like pressure switches or gauges that might need replacing. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after a complete on-site assessment.

    Common signs include your well pump repeatedly turning on and off (short cycling), fluctuating water pressure, air sputtering from faucets, or a tank that is waterlogged (feels heavy when tapped and full of water). If you notice any of these, it is time for an inspection.

    GNV Plumbing offers a solid warranty on both the well pressure tank itself (as per manufacturer's warranty) and our installation labor. We stand behind our work to ensure your complete satisfaction and peace of mind.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, well pressure tank replacement involves working with pressurized water, electrical components, and ensuring proper sizing and calibration. It is strongly recommended to hire a professional plumber to ensure safety, code compliance, and proper system function.

    A typical well pressure tank replacement usually takes between 2 to 4 hours. This includes disconnecting the old tank, installing the new one, and testing the system to ensure proper operation and correct pressure.

    Many residential tanks carry 5 to 7 year manufacturer warranties. We add our own labor warranty on the install.

    Both bladder and diaphragm tanks use an air cushion to pressurize water, but they differ in how the water and air are separated. Bladder tanks have a replaceable rubber bladder that holds the water, fully isolating it from the air. Diaphragm tanks use a fixed rubber diaphragm. Bladder tanks are often preferred for their longevity and ease of maintenance.
