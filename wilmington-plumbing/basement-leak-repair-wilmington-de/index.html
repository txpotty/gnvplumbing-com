    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Wilmington

    A basement floor drain that backs up during heavy use or a storm typically points to a blockage or a failed trap. We clear the drain, check the line to the main sewer and replace cracked traps and covers. Installing a backwater valve stops sewage from pushing back into the basement if the main line surcharges. We set the valve where it can be serviced easily.

    Sump Pump Repair and Installation in Wilmington, DE

    Many basements rely on a sump pit to handle storm runoff and groundwater. A high water table means heavy rain can push groundwater into crawlspaces and basements, making a working sump pump important. Pumps from brands like Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne fail most often from a stuck float or a burned-out motor after sitting idle for months. We repair or replace the pump, check the check valve and route the discharge well away from the foundation. A battery backup is worth considering because big storms often knock out power.

    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in Wilmington, DE

    Water heaters, softeners and laundry machines are often installed in basements, and their leaks pool quickly. If a tank has rusted through, we can replace it the same day in most situations.
