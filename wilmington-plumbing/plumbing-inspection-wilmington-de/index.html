    Detailed Plumbing Inspections in Wilmington, DE

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Wilmington, DE

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Wilmington, DE

    Older homes in Wilmington often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. Our camera shows each defect, and the onboard counter tells us exactly how far from the cleanout it sits. We mark it on the ground. Knowing the depth and location means a repair estimate can be accurate, and it helps decide between a spot repair, a liner or a full replacement.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Wilmington

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Our report tells buyers what pipe the house has, what the sewer looks like and how old the heater is, while there is still time to act. Findings help with negotiations. Old galvanized, a damaged sewer or an aging heater can all become a repair request or a closing credit.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Wilmington, DE

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Wilmington, DE

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. Plan on an hour or so. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Wilmington

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. We test the meter, watch for pressure loss and look for damp baseboards and mildew, and on slab homes we follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint the leak. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Wilmington

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. We check every visible pipe for corrosion and leaks, run each fixture for flow and drainage and test the water heater, the main shutoff and the angle stops. Pressure gets measured too. High pressure gets flagged.

Our pre-purchase inspection covers the supply pipe type, the sewer condition on video, the water heater's age and every fixture. We send the report to your agent too.
