    Shower Valve Replacement in Glasgow

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, reliable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves accessing the valve behind the shower wall.

    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Glasgow

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops. Your shower will hold a steady, consistent temperature.

    Faulty Shower Valve Repair in Glasgow

    Problems with your shower's water temperature or pressure often point to a malfunctioning mixing valve or cartridge. These components control the hot and cold water flow. Our technicians are skilled in diagnosing valve issues and can repair or replace them, ensuring you get consistent water temperature and pressure every time you shower. We work with all major valve brands.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Glasgow, DE

    Many homes in Glasgow feature Moen or Delta shower fixtures, known for their quality but still subject to wear and tear. Our plumbers are very experienced in repairing and sourcing parts for these popular brands. Whether it is a leaky single-handle cartridge or a diverter issue, we have the specialized knowledge to fix your Moen or Delta shower correctly.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Glasgow, DE

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure if needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed fast.

    Shower Drain Repair in Glasgow

    Slow shower drains are typically clogged with hair and soap scum, often hardened by mineral deposits. We clear the drain with augers and check the drain assembly for leaks. Loose or cracked drain bodies can let water seep under the pan. We repair or replace drain components when needed.

    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Glasgow

    A shower that drips after it is turned off usually has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn smoothly again.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Glasgow, DE

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in adjoining rooms.
