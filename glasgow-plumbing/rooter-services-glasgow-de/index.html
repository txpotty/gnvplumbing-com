    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Glasgow

    Tree roots are naturally drawn to water and nutrients, which they find in sewer lines through tiny cracks or loose joints. As roots grow, they can expand and completely block the pipe, a common issue in Glasgow neighborhoods with mature trees and older pipes.

    While chemical drain cleaners are available, GNV Plumbing generally advises against them. They often only partially clear clogs, can damage pipes over time, and pose safety risks. Professional rooter services are much more effective, safer for your plumbing, and provide a lasting fix to blockages.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a permanent fix.

    Snaking uses an auger to break through and pull out blockages, while hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire inside of the pipe, removing buildup like grease and mineral deposits. Hydro-jetting provides a far more thorough cleaning, often recommended for recurring clogs or main sewer lines.

    We use top professional-grade equipment, including Spartan Tool and Ridgid rooter machines and hydro-jetters. These brands are known for their power and durability, allowing us to tackle the toughest blockages without damaging your plumbing system.
