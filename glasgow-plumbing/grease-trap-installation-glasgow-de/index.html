    Two warranties apply here. The trap body carries the maker's coverage, which runs from one year on some steel units to much longer on plastic. Our labor warranty covers the joints and the setting, and you get it in writing up front.

    Plan on a permit. Grease interceptors are regulated by both the plumbing code and the local sewer use ordinance, so two offices may want to see the plans. You get copies of every approval for your files. Unpermitted traps cause trouble at lease transfer.

    We start with a fixture list and the drain rate of each fixture.
