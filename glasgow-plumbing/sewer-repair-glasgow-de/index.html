    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Glasgow

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Glasgow, DE

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip.
