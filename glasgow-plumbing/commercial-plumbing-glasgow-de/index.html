    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in Glasgow, DE

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Glasgow

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. We test reduced pressure zone, double check and pressure vacuum breaker assemblies with gauges calibrated every year and submit the results online or on paper, whichever your provider uses. Failed devices get rebuilt on the spot when we have the kit.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Glasgow, DE

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. When pinholes keep moving around the building, repiping the worst zones in Type L copper or PEX usually beats paying for more emergency visits. We map the system first. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Glasgow, DE

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless units get a descaling flush. If the building has a recirc loop, we test the pump and check valve too.

    Grease Trap Installation in Glasgow

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Small kitchens can get by with an under-sink trap, but bigger operations often need an in-ground interceptor of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We size the unit from the equipment schedule, set it at the right grade and vent it properly so it passes inspection the first time.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Glasgow, DE

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. We shut fixtures off zone by zone and watch the meter, then use acoustic sensors, thermal cameras and tracer gas to find leaks in walls, ceilings and buried service lines. Sometimes the culprit is just a running toilet. A single stuck flush valve can burn through thousands of gallons in a week.

    Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Glasgow

    A burst supply line above a drop ceiling can soak inventory and ceiling tile in under an hour. Shut the main first. Our dispatcher will walk your staff to the shutoff over the phone if needed, then send a plumber who can isolate the branch, restore water to the rest of the building and make the permanent repair. Backups get handled the same way. We clear the main, camera it and tell you whether it will happen again.
