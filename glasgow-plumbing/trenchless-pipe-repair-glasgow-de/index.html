    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Glasgow

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Glasgow yard.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Glasgow

    Older Glasgow homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Glasgow

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's built for sewer use. The liners and resins are structural, meaning the cured pipe can stand on its own even if the old pipe around it keeps deteriorating, which is why we trust them for local lines.
