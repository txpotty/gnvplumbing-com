    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Glasgow, DE

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without wasting salt or water.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Glasgow, DE

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Valve and Controller Diagnostics in Glasgow, DE

    The control valve and electronic controller regulate the entire softening process. Our technicians perform diagnostic checks to confirm these components are operating accurately, adjusting programming as needed. We check for wear and tear on moving parts and lubricate them to prevent future issues, keeping your water softener reliable in Glasgow, DE.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Glasgow

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain plainly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Glasgow, DE

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes prevent leaks and backflow problems.

    Hardness Test and Water Quality Check in Glasgow

    As part of our maintenance, we run a water hardness test to verify your water softener is effectively reducing mineral content to proper levels. This confirms you are receiving the soft water benefits you expect and allows us to fine-tune your system if necessary. We aim for consistent water quality in every Glasgow home we serve.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Glasgow, DE

    The brine tank is essential for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can accumulate, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a common issue we address in Glasgow homes.

    A standard water softener maintenance service typically takes about 1 to 2 hours. This can vary depending on the system's condition, age and whether any unforeseen issues are discovered during the inspection.

    Most visits take about an hour, including testing and a manual regeneration. Resin replacement can add another hour or a bit more.

    Resin often lasts 10 to 15 years, but chlorinated city water and iron can shorten that. Fine resin beads in your faucet aerators can signal resin breakdown.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms such as mineral spots on dishes, dry skin and hair, or scale buildup on fixtures. You might also notice your softener using more salt than usual, unusual noises or the system not regenerating properly. Don't wait for a complete failure.
