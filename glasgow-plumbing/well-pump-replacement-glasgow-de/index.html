    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Glasgow, DE

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. Pressure switches with burned contacts or a clogged pickup tube can leave the pump sitting idle while the tank drains to zero. The switch itself is a cheap part. You get to see the old part.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Glasgow, DE

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. The common failure is losing prime. Usually a leaking foot valve or a cracked suction line lets the water fall back down the well, so we check both before we blame the pump.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Glasgow, DE

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. We replace the pump and prime it properly. After that, we watch the pressure gauge cycle from cut-in to cut-out.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Glasgow

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. We replace it with schedule 120 PVC or poly pipe, depending on the depth and the pump weight. The wire gets checked too. Cracked insulation and taped splices get cut out.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Glasgow

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. We check the air charge with a gauge. The air charge should read 2 psi under the switch cut-in.
