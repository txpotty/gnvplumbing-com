    Navien Venting and Install in North Star

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in North Star, DE

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Shutting off and draining outdoor spigots in the fall and insulating crawlspace pipes does a lot toward preventing winter leaks. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Commercial Tankless Racks in North Star, DE

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. Four units together can pull over 600,000 BTU, which means checking gas pressure with all of them running. Installs can happen after closing.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in North Star

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Otherwise one unit does all the work. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in North Star, DE

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. One bad unit can come out while the others keep running. Hand sinks and dish stations have to hit the temperatures health code requires.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in North Star

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Shut the gas and cold isolation valve now. Then we look at whether the leak is a fitting or the heat exchanger itself, because one is a $20 fix and the other means a new unit.
