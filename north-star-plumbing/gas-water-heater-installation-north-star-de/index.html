    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in North Star

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas water heater usually costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless gas units often cost more due to venting and gas line requirements.

    A conventional gas water heater stores and heats a large volume of water in a tank, providing a limited supply. A gas tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit, offering an endless supply but requiring more complex venting and gas line work.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for gas water heater installation. We pull permits when required and make sure the work passes inspection.

    Power vent units use a fan to push exhaust outside, while direct vent units use a sealed system that draws outside air. Both allow flexible placement compared to atmospheric vents.

    Gas heaters usually recover faster and can cost less to operate where gas rates are reasonable. Electric units are simpler and do not require venting.

    The main difference is the type of gas they use and the burners and orifices required for combustion. Natural gas is typically supplied by a utility line, while propane is stored in a tank. We install both types and confirm correct setup for your fuel source.

    Rusty water, leaks, pilot problems and slow recovery are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement may be the smarter option.

    New gas water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components. GNV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services, giving you peace of mind.
