    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in North Star, DE

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Water in Delaware ranges from moderately soft to hard depending on the source, and groundwater areas often see more scale on fixtures. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.
