    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Brookside

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field fast. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where it makes sense.

    Septic Pump and Alarm System Repair in Brookside

    Many modern septic systems, especially those on sloped properties or with advanced treatment units, rely on a working septic pump and alarm. When your pump fails or the alarm sounds, it indicates a serious issue. Our team can repair or replace faulty pumps, floats and electrical components so your system effectively moves wastewater to the drain field. We service all major pump brands for homes in Brookside.

    Septic Inlet Line Repair in Brookside

    The line from your house to the tank can crack, sag or separate from soil movement and roots. Symptoms include gurgling drains and backups even after pumping.
