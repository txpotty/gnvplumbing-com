    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Claymont

    Pressure-assist toilets from Sloan Flushmate and dual-flush models from TOTO and American Standard use parts that hardware stores seldom stock. We carry cartridges, flush buttons and seals for the common models and can order specialty parts quickly.     Running Toilet Repair in Claymont

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to much higher water bills. This common issue is often caused by a faulty flapper, a misaligned fill valve, or an improperly adjusted float. Our plumbers quickly identify the exact cause, whether it's mineral buildup from hard water affecting the flapper seal or a worn-out component, and replace the necessary parts to stop the continuous flow and restore efficiency.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Claymont

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet much quieter.

    Leaking Toilet Repair in Claymont

    Toilet leaks can cause significant water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can originate from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. Common culprits include a worn-out wax ring, loose bolts, or cracked porcelain. We carefully inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Claymont, DE

    Water around the base usually means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a fresh seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Claymont, DE

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem may be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.
