    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Claymont, DE

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Claymont, DE

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Claymont

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. Most need an access panel or a back-wall opening. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Same-Day Tub Faucet Replacement in Claymont, DE

    Some faucet failures cannot wait. Snapped handles and broken spouts get same-day visits when a plumber is free. Shut off the water first. Leave the tub drain open.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Claymont, DE

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Many Delta Monitor valves accept a new cartridge and trim from the front, and the MultiChoice universal rough-in lets you switch trim styles later without touching the pipes in the wall. Popular Delta cartridges are on the truck. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Claymont, DE

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. Unlike a pressure-balance valve, which only reacts to pressure changes, a thermostatic valve senses the actual water temperature and adjusts the mix, which matters for kids and older adults who cannot react fast to a hot spike. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Tub Faucet Style Upgrades in Claymont

    Wall-mount tub fillers are a popular upgrade. A long-reach filler or a slide-bar handheld changes the tub, and both usually need a new valve or diverter. We plan the rough-in first. The trim goes on last.

    Tub Valves in Older Houses in Claymont, DE

    Cast iron tubs and plaster walls change the plan. Plaster cracks wider than drywall when you cut it, so we open plaster carefully with a multi-tool and often go in from an adjacent closet, and the heavy tub stays put while we work on the valve above it. Patching plaster is slow. Access panels avoid it.
