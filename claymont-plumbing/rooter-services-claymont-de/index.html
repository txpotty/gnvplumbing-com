    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Claymont

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet typically runs $125 to $300. Main sewer rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. With hydro jetting added, expect roughly $450 to $1,000, depending on how long the line is and how heavy the grease is. After-hours calls cost more.

    Rental machines are easy to get, but they are dangerous in untrained hands. A cable can kink and wrap a hand or wrist fast. They also get stuck. A rental cable stuck in an old clay line can turn a $300 rooter call into a dig to retrieve it.

    Slow drains, gurgling pipes and sewer smell are the early ones. If the tub fills when the toilet flushes, the main is blocked. That one needs a call. Several fixtures acting up at once almost always points to a main line clog rather than a single trap under one sink.

    Clearing a clog in Claymont does not need a permit. Repairs are another matter. A new cleanout, a dig to fix broken pipe or a sewer replacement usually needs a plumbing permit. We handle the permit and the inspection.

    Drain snaking, also called augering, uses a flexible cable and cutting head to break up or pull out the clog. It works well on hair, wipes and roots. Jetting scours the whole pipe with high-pressure water and strips the grease, scale and sludge a cable slides past. Repeat clogs usually need jetting.

    Chemical drain openers rarely clear a real clog and can eat at old pipe. Old cast iron and the thin brass traps found in Claymont's older homes suffer the worst of it. They also make our job riskier. Let us know if you used a chemical, because that water splashes when the trap comes off.

    A limited guarantee for a set number of days comes with most rooter work. If the line clogs again within that time, we return at no extra charge.
