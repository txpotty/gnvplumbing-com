    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Claymont

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks. It is the usual pick for low-hazard lines such as many fire sprinklers, and because it does not discharge water, it can go in a vault where local rules allow it, sized so it does not rob your system of pressure in Claymont. We leave room for the tester.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Claymont, DE

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Claymont, DE. They let air in when supply pressure drops, which breaks the siphon, but they only protect against back-siphonage, so they must sit above every downstream outlet and cannot be used where back pressure is possible. Clearance and drainage get checked as well.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Claymont

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. When pressure between the checks gets too close to supply pressure, the relief opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Claymont. We install Watts and Febco units.

They have two spring-loaded checks and test cocks for annual certification, and since they do not discharge water, they can go in a vault or below grade where local rules allow, sized so the pressure loss stays small.
