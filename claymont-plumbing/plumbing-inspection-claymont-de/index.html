    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Claymont, DE

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Claymont, DE

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Claymont, DE

    Salt air in coastal parts of Delaware speeds corrosion on outdoor fittings, hose bibs, and exterior metal pipe. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. Insulation gets checked too. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Claymont

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Commercial Property Plumbing Checks in Claymont

    Businesses in Claymont use our inspections to plan maintenance instead of reacting to breakdowns. Backflow devices, heaters, flush valves, floor drains, trap primers and the sewer all get checked. Closures are expensive. With a ranked report, repairs get scheduled after close instead of forcing a shutdown.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Claymont, DE

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. We check for old uncoated brass connectors, missing shutoffs, missing sediment traps and corrosion, and we test joints with an electronic detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Older homes are where we find the most issues, usually at appliance connectors that have been there since the house was built.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Claymont, DE

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. Plan on an hour or so. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging.     Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Claymont

    A standard plumbing inspection in Claymont, DE usually costs $150 to $350. Size changes the price. The base price covers pipe, fixtures, the heater, drains and pressure, and the sewer camera is usually a separate line since it adds time and equipment. You get the price when you book.

    Our full inspection covers supply pipe, drains, fixtures, shutoff valves, the water heater, gas connectors and the sewer line. Pressure gets measured. We also run a meter leak test, look for signs of slab leaks and hard water damage and check any softener or filter system tied into the plumbing.

    Watch for a water bill that jumps for no reason, low pressure, slow drains or brown water. Noisy drains are a clue. Damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell under a sink or a warm patch of tile can all point to a hidden leak that an inspection will find before it does more damage.

    No permit is needed for an inspection. Repairs may need one. Permits come into play for repairs, replacements or new installations that follow from the inspection, and if any of those apply, we will tell you up front and handle the paperwork if we do the work.

    Most residential inspections in Claymont take one to two hours. Age and size matter. The exact time depends on how big the house is, how old the plumbing is and whether we find something that needs extra tracing, such as a hidden leak or a sewer defect.

    We can include estimated repair ranges in the report and firm quotes if you want us to do the work. You are never required to hire us.

    A one to two year routine works for most homes. Older homes or those with past leaks do well with yearly checks.

    We inspect all the common brands and materials. That includes Rheem, AO Smith and Bradford White heaters. We work with PEX, copper, CPVC, PVC, cast iron, galvanized and poly, and we know the usual weak points for each and for common fixture brands.
