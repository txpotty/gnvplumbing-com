    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Claymont, DE

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an efficient and popular choice for preserving property appearance in Claymont.
