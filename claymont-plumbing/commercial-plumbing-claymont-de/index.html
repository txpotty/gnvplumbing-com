    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in Claymont, DE

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. We can also swap in lower-flow aerators that still rinse hands well. Most fixture calls are done in under two hours.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Claymont, DE

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. Running flush valves, stuck urinals and dripping sensor faucets usually need a diaphragm, a solenoid or fresh batteries. We stock those. Wall-hung toilets that keep clogging or rocking get a carrier check, since a loose bowl can leak inside the wall. Low pressure on one fixture usually points to a clogged supply stop.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Claymont

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. Most kitchens see the same drain back up every few months. We open the line with a sectional cable, then jet the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so the grease does not just close back up in a week. We also check trap primers.

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Claymont, DE

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property. First we run a camera and locate the damage depth, then we compare a spot dig, a cured-in-place liner and pipe bursting. Trenchless saves parking lots. Lining a 6-inch lateral can often be done in a day without tearing up asphalt or closing an entrance.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Claymont, DE

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. We check draft and combustion, test water temperature at the far end of the building and flush the tank, then tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense. Tankless racks get descaled. If the building has a recirc loop, we test the pump and check valve too.

    Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Claymont

    A burst supply line above a drop ceiling can soak inventory and ceiling tile in under an hour. Shut the main first. Our dispatcher will walk your staff to the shutoff over the phone if needed, then send a plumber who can isolate the branch, restore water to the rest of the building and make the permanent repair. Backups get handled the same way. After clearing the line we run a camera so you know whether it is a one-time clog or damaged pipe.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Claymont

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. We push the camera from a cleanout, note the footage at every break, root mass or sag, and use a locator to mark depth and position on the ground above. The video is yours to keep. That record makes capital planning far easier when the board asks why the sewer needs money.

    Grease Trap Installation in Claymont
