    Shower Diverter Repair in Claymont, DE

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. It is a short job.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Claymont
