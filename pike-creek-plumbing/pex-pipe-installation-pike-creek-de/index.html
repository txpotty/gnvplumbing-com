    Why Choose GNV Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Pike Creek, DE?

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Pike Creek

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. No crimp tool to calibrate. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Pike Creek

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.
