    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Pike Creek, DE

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. It does not take much debris to block it.     Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Pike Creek

    Resin does the actual softening. Each bead trades sodium for calcium and magnesium until it runs out of capacity. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to see whether the bed still holds its rated capacity.

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Pike Creek

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. In between, we check the salt level, the brine well and the float. Figure on an hour for a typical unit.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Pike Creek, DE

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Pike Creek

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. If it hits a hard layer and then falls through, there is a bridge. We break the crust, pull out the mushy salt underneath with a wet-dry vac, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Service for Most Softener Brands in Pike Creek, DE
