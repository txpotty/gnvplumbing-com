    Most standard toilet repairs, such as fixing a running toilet or replacing internal tank components, can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex issues like clearing a stubborn main line clog or replacing a wax ring might take longer, potentially 2 to 4 hours.

    Swapping a flapper is a fair DIY job if you match the right size and the flush valve seat is smooth. Base leaks, flange repairs and cracked tanks are better left to a plumber because a bad reset can cause hidden water damage to the subfloor.

    Most flapper, fill valve and supply line repairs take 20 to 45 minutes. Pulling and resetting a toilet typically takes about an hour, and flange repairs can add another 30 to 60 minutes.

    If your toilet is overflowing, right away reach behind the toilet and turn the small valve on the water supply line clockwise to shut off the water.
