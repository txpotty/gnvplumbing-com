    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Pike Creek, DE

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard.     Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Pike Creek, DE

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Urine leaves a hard mineral buildup inside the trap and the drain line, especially on waterless units and low-flow fixtures that send less water through, so we slope the line properly and add a cleanout where we can. Venting stops gurgle and odor. We tie in within code distance.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Pike Creek

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Rusted bolts and seized valves often have to be cut, so we protect the tile while working. We never reuse the old gasket. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.
