    Many homes benefit from a softener when the water is hard. Private wells are common in rural Delaware, and they can carry iron, nitrates, or low pH that calls for testing and treatment. Softeners protect water heaters, fixtures and appliances from scale.

    Homeowners with a loop can sometimes install a softener, but proper drain air gaps, bypass valves and programming matter. Professional installation helps avoid leaks and poor performance.

    While some homeowners attempt DIY water softener installation, it's highly recommended to hire a professional plumber.     Common signs of hard water include soap scum buildup on fixtures and shower doors, dry skin and hair, dull laundry and scale buildup inside pipes and appliances like water heaters. We can perform a water hardness test in your Pike Creek home to verify your need for a softener.
