    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Pike Creek, DE

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first.     Basement Wall Sealing in Pike Creek, DE

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Humidity causes condensation on cold water lines and toilet tanks, which can slowly damage floors and encourage mold. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.
