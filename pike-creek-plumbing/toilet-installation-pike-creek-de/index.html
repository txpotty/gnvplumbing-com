    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Pike Creek, DE

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. Cast iron flanges rust through at the bolt slots.     ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Pike Creek

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. It is set that way so a grab bar fits.     Urgent Toilet Replacement in Pike Creek

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. Standard two-piece toilets ride on our trucks.     Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Pike Creek

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Pike Creek, DE

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Pike Creek

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring.
