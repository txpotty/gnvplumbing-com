    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in New Castle

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in New Castle

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems.     Leak Detection and Repair in New Castle, DE

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.
