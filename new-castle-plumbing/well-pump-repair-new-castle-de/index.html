    Well Pressure Tank Repair in New Castle

    The pressure tank works with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. Issues like a waterlogged tank, sudden pressure drops, or continuous pump operation often point to a faulty pressure tank. We inspect the air bladder, pressure switch, and tank connections to find the problem. In New Castle, we repair or replace tanks to optimize your well system's performance and extend pump life.

    Pressure Tank Repair in New Castle, DE

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps prematurely. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives steadier pressure at the tap.

    Jet Pump Repair in New Castle, DE

    Jet pumps, typically installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Common issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team in New Castle is skilled at restoring prime and replacing worn components to get your jet pump operating smoothly again, a frequent need where water levels rise and fall.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in New Castle

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms often include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use dedicated equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, protecting the integrity of your well system.
