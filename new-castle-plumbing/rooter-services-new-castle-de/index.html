    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in New Castle

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet usually costs $125 to $300. Main sewer rooting through a cleanout generally runs $200 to $500. With hydro jetting added, expect roughly $450 to $1,000, depending on how long the line is and how heavy the grease is. Nights and weekends run higher.

    Most single-drain clogs take 1 to 3 hours from arrival to flow test. A root-packed main or a job that adds jetting can take most of a day. Access makes the biggest difference. With no usable cleanout, we pull a toilet to get in, and that adds about an hour.

    Snaking runs a spinning cable with a cutting head through the clog. A jetter uses water at thousands of psi to scour the inside of the pipe from wall to wall. Jetting cleans more. We usually jet recurring grease lines and scaled mains, and snake the one-off clogs.

    Watch for very slow drains, gurgling from toilets and sour odors. Water coming up in the shower when something else drains is a classic main line symptom. Act on that quickly. Waiting raises the odds that the next flush puts sewage on the floor.

    Sewer pipes leak a little water vapor at the joints. Roots chase that moisture to the joint and work their way inside. Inside, they thicken fast. In New Castle, clay pipe with a joint every 2 to 3 feet and older cast iron with oakum joints are the most common targets.

    Rental machines are easy to get, but they are dangerous in untrained hands. A cable can kink and wrap a hand or wrist fast. Rentals jam in pipes. Getting a rental cable stuck in clay pipe can mean digging it out, which costs far more than a rooter visit.

    It can be, so we prepare for it. Drop cloths go down from the door to the work area, and we keep buckets and rags at the cleanout. The cable comes back dirty. It gets wiped as it comes out, and debris goes into bags before we clean up and leave.
