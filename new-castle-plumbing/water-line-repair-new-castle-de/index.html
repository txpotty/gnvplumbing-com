    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in New Castle, DE

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Near the coast around New Castle, some shallow wells pick up a salty taste, which can wear on pipes and water heaters faster. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in New Castle

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in New Castle, DE

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time.
