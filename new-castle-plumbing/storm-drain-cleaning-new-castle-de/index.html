    Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in New Castle, DE

    Hydro jetting is a proven solution for deeply clogged storm drains in New Castle, DE. This method uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup. It is highly effective for thoroughly cleaning the inside of the pipe walls, restoring the drain to its full capacity and preventing a quick return of blockages, a common issue with older drainage systems.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in New Castle

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in New Castle, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can work into drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. GNV Plumbing uses specialized root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can suggest further steps to prevent future root problems.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in New Castle, DE

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. We jet them clear and test with a hose to confirm flow at the outlet. For flat roofs with scuppers, we check that the discharge path is open too. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and cracking.

    Channel and Trench Drain Cleaning in New Castle

    Channel drains across driveways and garage entries catch water before it reaches the house. Their narrow channels fill quickly with sand, leaves and debris. We remove the grates, clean the channel and jet the outlet pipe. Cracked or loose grates can be replaced while we are there. A clean trench drain keeps water out of your garage during a downpour.

    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in New Castle

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can carry a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and seated.
