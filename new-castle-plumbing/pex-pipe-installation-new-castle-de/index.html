    Why Choose GNV Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in New Castle, DE?

    Manufacturer rules for PEX are specific: supports about every 32 inches on horizontal runs, a minimum bend radius, and no long sun exposure. We follow them on every run. Ignoring them causes kinks, sags and strained fittings, and it can cost you the manufacturer warranty that should have backed the pipe.

    • Lower Install Cost, PEX is cheaper than copper by the foot and takes fewer labor hours.
    • Quieter Lines, PEX softens water hammer and carries less rushing sound than copper.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in New Castle, DE

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines. Each fixture gets its own line from a central header, and each line gets its own valve, so you can shut off one toilet without killing water to the kitchen. It uses more pipe. The trade-off is almost no fittings hidden in the walls and steadier pressure when two showers run at once.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in New Castle

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. There is no crimp gauge to check. Because the fittings are wide inside, each joint costs very little flow.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in New Castle

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. You get real pressure back, and the discolored water goes away with the old pipe.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in New Castle

    The main from the meter to the house is the one line everything else depends on. A leaking old service line shows up as a soggy patch in the yard, a higher water bill and weak pressure everywhere. We trench and lay new PEX. At the house we add a new full-port ball valve for the main shutoff.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in New Castle, DE

    Price on a repipe depends on a short list: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX usually beats copper on price because the pipe costs less and a crew finishes in two to four days, not a full week. Patching is sometimes quoted on its own line. We price PEX-A, PEX-B and copper side by side when you ask.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in New Castle

    Low pressure at every faucet, not just one, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In older homes it is rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with failing fittings, and swapping a faucet cartridge will not help. A whole-home repipe will. We replace every supply line from the main valve to each angle stop and test the system before closing anything.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in New Castle, DE

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.
