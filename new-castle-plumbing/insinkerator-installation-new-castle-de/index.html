    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in New Castle, DE

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. Because of that height, the discharge can end up below the trap arm, and we measure first so we can lower the trap if needed. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in New Castle

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    New Construction Disposal Installs in New Castle

    New builds and gut remodels get the disposal in two trips. During rough-in, the drain stub height gets set and the electrician leaves a switched outlet in the sink cabinet.     Evolution Upgrade Installs in New Castle, DE

    Quiet is the main thing people notice after an Evolution install. Insulation around the motor drops the noise a lot.
