    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in New Castle, DE

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. Replace the sink only if the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain has rusted into the bowl. When it is close, you get both prices. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in New Castle

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. Older compression faucets need a washer and seat, and newer cartridge or disc faucets need the cartridge itself or its seals. We carry Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler cartridges. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in New Castle, DE

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. A clogged aerator screen causes most low flow at a single faucet, and if cleaning it does not help we check the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Low pressure everywhere points to a different problem. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in New Castle

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Freezing weather arrives most winters in Delaware, and pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls, and unheated garages are the most likely to freeze. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in New Castle, DE

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in New Castle, DE

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We run a camera or a longer cable into the wall arm and look for a sagging line or buildup where the sink ties into the tub drain. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Sink Parts Replacement in New Castle, DE

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in New Castle

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.
