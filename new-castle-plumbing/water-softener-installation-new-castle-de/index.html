    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in New Castle

    The cost of water softener installation in New Castle, DE typically ranges from $1,500 to $4,000, depending on the system type, brand and complexity of the installation.     Maintenance mainly involves periodically adding salt to salt-based systems, typically every 4 to 8 weeks depending on water usage and hardness.
