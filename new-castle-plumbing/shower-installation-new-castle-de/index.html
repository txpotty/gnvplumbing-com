    New Construction Shower Installation in New Castle, DE

    New homes get their shower plumbing during framing. Valves, supplies and drains go in at framing, with valve depth matched to the finish and drains set for your pan. Inspection comes next. We return for trim after tile.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in New Castle, DE

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. We add solid wood blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare, set the valve within reach of a seat and install a handheld on a slide bar at a height that works seated or standing. No high curbs. ADA layouts are available on request.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in New Castle

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in New Castle

    Not every upgrade needs demolition. A new pan, a tub conversion or a new valve and trim can change the bathroom without a gut job. We price the options side by side. They tend to wrap up within a week.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in New Castle

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Then we test. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in New Castle, DE

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. On a slab, we cut concrete. The new shower is safer to step into.

    Shower Pan Installation in New Castle

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. We level prefab pans carefully. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in New Castle

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. We install heavy-duty commercial valves, push-button or metered controls when you want to limit run time, and drains sized for several showers at once. ADA stalls get handheld units. We phase the work to keep some showers running.
