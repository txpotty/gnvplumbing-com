    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Millsboro, DE

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Millsboro

    The cost of PEX pipe installation in Millsboro, DE varies with the scope of the project, such as a full repipe or a small repair. Generally, homeowners can expect to pay between $3,000 and $10,000 for a whole-home repipe, while smaller projects might range from $500 to $2,000. We provide detailed, transparent estimates before starting any work.

    A full home PEX repipe for an average-sized home in Millsboro usually takes 3 to 5 days. Smaller projects or single-line replacements can often be completed in a single day. We aim to minimize disruption and communicate timelines clearly.

    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has fewer joints. Copper is very durable but can develop pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last many decades. Manufacturers often offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    Small repairs are possible for handy homeowners, but a full repipe requires proper sizing, support spacing and code compliance. Mistakes behind walls can cause hidden leaks and failed inspections.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light breaks down it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or completely covered.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not develop pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Millsboro homes. It also reduces water hammer noise.

    We install high-quality PEX piping from reputable manufacturers such as Uponor (PEX-A), Viega (PEX-B) and Zurn (PEX-B). We also work with other trusted brands like SharkBite for fittings, so the best materials are used for your installation.
