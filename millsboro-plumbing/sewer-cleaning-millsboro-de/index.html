    Tree Root Removal in Millsboro, DE Sewers

    Tree roots are a common culprit of sewer line blockages, especially in Millsboro, DE neighborhoods where mature trees are common. GNV Plumbing uses dedicated root cutting tools attached to our drain augers and high-pressure hydro jetting to remove these invasive roots, restoring flow and preventing further damage.     Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Millsboro, DE

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Older homes in Delaware may still have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains, both of which corrode with age. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall.
