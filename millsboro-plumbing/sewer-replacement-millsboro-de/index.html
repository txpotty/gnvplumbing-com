    Sewer Line Replacement Methods in Millsboro, DE

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Millsboro, DE

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. Lining patches the inside of the old one. We use bursting when the old line is broken or undersized, since it puts a new pipe in place instead of reinforcing the old, and lining when the pipe is still round and mostly intact. Each has its place.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Millsboro, DE

    Trenching lets us fix grade. That is its big advantage. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, while a trench lets us lay new pipe at the right pitch. Bellies disappear.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Millsboro

    Digging is still the right method for a lot of lines. If the old pipe has caved in, trenchless has nothing to follow. Sharp bends, a belly that needs regrading, or a route that should change all point toward an open trench, and we leave the yard as close to how we found it as dirt allows. Grass grows back.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Millsboro

    Bigger pipe, more flow, more rules. Most commercial lines are 6-inch or larger. We handle the permit, set up bypass pumping when the building cannot shut down, and arrange traffic control when the work reaches the road, keeping the outage as short as the job allows. Night shifts possible.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Millsboro

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. Inspectors check all three in an open trench. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and only bury pipe rated for it. Paperwork stays on file.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Millsboro

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all failed for different reasons. Modern PVC does not rust, flatten or let roots in at glued joints. Schedule 40 or SDR 35 PVC is what most codes call for on a lateral, and we match the grade to the depth and load over the pipe. Glued joints stay sealed.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Millsboro, DE

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. We dig a launch pit near the house and a receiving pit near the main. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, which puts new pipe in the same spot without digging the yard. Driveways stay put.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Millsboro

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. There are two options. We can cut and remove a strip of floor inside the house and patch it after, or dig a tunnel beneath the foundation from the yard, and the right choice depends on the length of the run, the flooring, and what sits above the pipe.
