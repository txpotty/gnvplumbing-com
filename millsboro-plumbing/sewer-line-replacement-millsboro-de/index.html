    Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in Millsboro, DE

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, and Orangeburg fiber pipe flattens and blisters with age. Both are near the end of their service life in many older properties. When the camera shows multiple failures, replacement is typically the best value. We remove or burst the old line and install modern pipe with a long expected service life. You get a smooth, root-resistant line out to the main.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Millsboro, DE

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the proper grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we close it up.

    Sewer Line Inspection and Diagnostics in Millsboro

    Before any replacement, a thorough video inspection is essential. We use high-resolution cameras to travel through your sewer lines, identifying the exact location and nature of the problem, whether it is cracks, root intrusion, or collapsed sections. This precision lets us recommend the most appropriate and cost-effective replacement plan. That way we address the root cause of your sewer issues in Millsboro.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in Millsboro, DE

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may solve the problem.     Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Millsboro

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is installed and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a good option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you plainly if your line is a candidate.
