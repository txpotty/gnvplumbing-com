    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Millsboro

    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Much of Delaware sits on sandy coastal plain soil with a high water table, which affects slab, crawlspace, and drainage design.     Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Millsboro

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Millsboro yard.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Millsboro, DE

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It also upsizes lines. Our crew sets an entry pit and an exit pit, then pulls a bursting head and new HDPE through on a hydraulic winch, which replaces the full run in a day or two for most homes. GNV Plumbing fuses the HDPE into one length.
