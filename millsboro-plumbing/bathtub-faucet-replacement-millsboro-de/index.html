    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Millsboro

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. Single-handle drips mean worn cartridge seals, and multi-handle drips mean worn washers and seats. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Upgrading Tub Faucets in Millsboro, DE

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks. Old cross handles can become a modern lever trim, and on a universal valve the wall never opens. We can match your sink finish. Delta and Moen make this simplest.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Millsboro, DE

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Millsboro

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. Most need an access panel or a back-wall opening. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Tub Faucet Style Upgrades in Millsboro

    Wall-mount tub fillers are a popular upgrade. Swapping a standard spout for a long-reach filler or a handheld shower on a slide bar changes how the tub works, and both usually need a new valve or a diverter added inside the wall. Rough-in gets planned before trim. The trim goes on last.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Millsboro

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck-mount fillers sit on the rim. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Emergency Tub Faucet Service in Millsboro, DE

    A tub that will not shut off needs water off now. Check behind the trim plate or an access panel for stops, and if there are none, shut the main and leave the drain open. We send the next open plumber. Broken stems are a common cause.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Millsboro, DE

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.
