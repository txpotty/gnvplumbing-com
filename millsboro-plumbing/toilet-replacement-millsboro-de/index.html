    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Millsboro

    Most toilet replacements, including a quality standard toilet, labor and disposal, cost $450 to $950. Premium one-piece or smart toilets can raise the total to $1,200 to $2,500. Flange repairs, if needed, usually add $100 to $250.

    A like-for-like toilet replacement in the same location generally does not require a permit. If drains are moved or a new bathroom is added, a permit is usually required.

    Key signs include persistent leaks that cannot be sealed, a cracked bowl or tank, frequent clogging despite repairs, or an old toilet that uses too much water. If your toilet is constantly running or has visible damage, replacement is usually the more cost-effective long-term solution.

    Frequent clogs, cracks in the porcelain, constant repairs, rocking that returns after resetting and very high water use are common signs. Toilets more than 25 years old are often good candidates for replacement.

    Older toilets can use 3.5 to 7 gallons per flush, while WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons or less. For a typical household, that can mean thousands of gallons saved each year.

    Signs of failing internal toilet parts include a toilet that runs constantly, a weak or incomplete flush, a handle that needs jiggling, or visible wear and tear on components like the flapper, fill valve, or flush valve. While some parts can be replaced one at a time, an older toilet with multiple failing components might benefit more from a full replacement.

    The rough-in measurement refers to the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drainpipe (the bolts that secure the toilet).
