    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Millsboro

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which one drops over fifteen to thirty minutes. The gauge points us to the right line. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Millsboro, DE home.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Millsboro, DE

    Under-slab drains crack as well.     Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Millsboro, DE

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Millsboro, DE home.
