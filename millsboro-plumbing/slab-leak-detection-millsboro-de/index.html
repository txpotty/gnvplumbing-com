    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Millsboro, DE

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair.     Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Millsboro, DE

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic.
