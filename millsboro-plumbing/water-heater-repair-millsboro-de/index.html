    Combustion is the part of a gas heater that matters most for safety. Yellow tips on the flame or soot around the draft hood point to a burner starved for air. We clean the burner and check draft with a mirror or a smoke test. Backdrafting is dangerous.

    Electric Water Heater Element Repair in Millsboro

    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. Each element gets an ohm reading. Readings tell us whether an element is open or shorted, which explains the breaker trips. Parts for most tanks ride on the truck.

    Leaking Water Heater Repair in Millsboro, DE

    Not every leak means a dead tank. Relief valve, drain valve and supply line drips are cheap fixes. Water from the tank body or bottom seam usually means the lining has failed, and replacement is the only real fix. We protect your floors either way.
