    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Millsboro, DE

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Millsboro

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Millsboro

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. It sits behind the toilet, grinds waste and pumps it through a small line, often three-quarter or one inch, to a drain above, which suits a small half bath. Venting still applies.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Millsboro

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. They cost more. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.
