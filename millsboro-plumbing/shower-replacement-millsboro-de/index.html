    Accessible Shower Replacement in Millsboro, DE

    Barrier-free showers need low or flat entries and controls within reach. We install the valve where someone seated can reach it, add blocking for grab bars and set a handheld on a slide bar, all following ADA guidelines when you ask for them. Entries stay wide. We plan for a seat.

    Rental and Hotel Shower Replacement in Millsboro

    Rental showers need to be durable and easy to fix. We replace valves, heads and units with commercial-grade parts and keep the cartridges consistent across units so future repairs take one part and one trip. We work around tenants. Between tenants is ideal.

    Old Shower Valve Replacement in Millsboro

    Two- and three-handle valves from decades back often leak and lack scald protection. Working from behind the wall when possible, we pull the old valve, install a new one and use a large trim plate to hide the old holes. Usually no new tile. It is a big safety upgrade.

    Fiberglass Shower Unit Replacement in Millsboro

    Narrow doorways change which unit we can use. A one-piece fiberglass shower often will not fit through a standard bathroom door, so we usually install a multi-piece unit that goes in as panels and seals at the seams. Both last a long time. We check the framing and drain before setting it.

    Aging-in-Place Shower Replacement in Millsboro

    Aging in place often starts with getting rid of the tub. We remove it, adjust the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height, with blocking for grab bars and a handheld shower on a slide bar so the shower works seated or standing. Thresholds stay low. It makes a real difference.

    Shower Pan Replacement in Millsboro, DE

    Cracked pans damage subfloors, walls and nearby rooms. With slab floors, water can move under flooring into the next room, so we trace it first. Then the pan comes out. The new pan gets proper slope, waterproofing and a flood test.

    Walk-In Shower Conversion in Millsboro, DE

    Walk-in conversions are one of the most requested bathroom jobs we do. The drain moves to the pan's center, the valve goes up to shower height and the slope keeps water from escaping the entry. Grab bar blocking is easy now. Accessibility standards are followed on request.

    Tub-to-Shower Replacement in Millsboro

    Old bathtubs that nobody uses take up the best spot in the bathroom. We remove the tub, reroute the drain to the new pan location and install the valve at shower height, then set walls and a door or glass panel. Stepping in gets easier. It is safer for everyone.
