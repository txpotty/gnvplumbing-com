    New Sump Pump Installation in Millsboro

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. Most of our installs get a cast iron pump, a check valve and 1-1/2 inch discharge. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It has to discharge outside, far enough from the foundation that it does not cycle back into the pit, often ten feet or more.

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Millsboro

    A sump pump sits in a pit below the slab and kicks on when a float rises. Most failures come from a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor, or a check valve that lets the discharge water fall back into the pit and short-cycles the pump. We test yours with a bucket. Add a battery backup if storms knock out power.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Millsboro, DE

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes rust through from the inside over decades. A wet spot at the base of the stack or a crack along the hub is common, and we replace the damaged section with PVC or new cast iron joined with no-hub couplings. Plastic joints can fail too. Loose PVC joints usually trace back to poor support or a fitting that was never fully seated.
