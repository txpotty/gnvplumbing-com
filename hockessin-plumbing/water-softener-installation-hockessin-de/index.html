    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Hockessin

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Water Softener Replacement in Hockessin, DE

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Hockessin

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds.
