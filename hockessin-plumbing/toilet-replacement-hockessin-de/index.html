    Types of Toilet Replacements in Hockessin, DE

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Hockessin, DE

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Hockessin, DE

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Closing the angle stop stops the water. We can usually replace it the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Hockessin, DE

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for a score of at least 800. A high score means fewer double flushes and fewer clogs, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Hockessin

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. The drain stays the same. We often pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Hockessin

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. Battery and hardwired models are both available. Work can happen after hours, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Hockessin

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. It needs to be replaced. We pull the cracked toilet and check the floor for water damage before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.

    Emergency Toilet Replacement in Hockessin

    A toilet that cracked overnight and is still seeping into the floor cannot wait for next week, so we bring a standard round and elongated model on emergency calls. Shut the angle stop first. Most of the time the new toilet goes in that same day, and we check the subfloor for soaked plywood before setting it.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Hockessin, DE

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.
