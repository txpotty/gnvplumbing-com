    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Hockessin, DE

    The inlet and outlet baffles inside your septic tank are vital for separating solids from liquids and keeping them out of the drain field. Over time, these baffles can deteriorate, leading to system failure. GNV Plumbing provides professional repair and replacement of damaged septic tank baffles, using durable materials that hold up to the harsh conditions within the tank and protect your entire septic system in Hockessin, DE.

    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Hockessin

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where it makes sense.
