    Acoustic ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and pressure gauges go on the truck for every leak call we run. Every tool confirms a different leak type. Using several together gives a reliable result.

    Most homes take between one and three hours. The time depends on where the leak is, how bad it is, how much of the house has to be tested and the type of pipe. Leaks under a slab can run a little longer, since sound travels differently through concrete.

    Watch for a water bill that jumps without a change in use, damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell, or water running with every fixture off. A moving meter is the clearest sign. Turn off every fixture and watch it for ten minutes.
