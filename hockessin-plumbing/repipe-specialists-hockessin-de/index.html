    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A with ProPEX fittings and Type L copper from domestic mills. We also use Viega ProPress fittings for copper repipes.

    Common signs you need repiping include persistent low water pressure throughout your home, discolored or rusty-looking water, frequent pipe leaks, visible corrosion on exposed pipes, and a metallic taste or odor in your water.     Both PEX and copper are strong choices for repiping in Delaware, each with distinct advantages. PEX is more flexible, resistant to freezing, and generally less expensive, making it a great option for many homes. Copper is highly durable, has a longer lifespan, and naturally inhibits bacterial growth, but costs more. We can discuss which material best suits your budget and needs.

    Repiping is a complex plumbing project that requires specialized tools, knowledge of plumbing codes, and real experience.     Water in Delaware ranges from moderately soft to hard depending on the source, and groundwater areas often see more mineral buildup on fixtures. Repiping with materials like PEX or properly installed copper, along with considering a water softener, can reduce these effects and extend the life of your plumbing system.
