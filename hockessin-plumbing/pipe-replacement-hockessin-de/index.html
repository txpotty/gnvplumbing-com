    PEX Pipe Replacement in Hockessin

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a popular choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. GNV Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials.
