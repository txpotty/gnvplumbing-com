    Why Polybutylene Fails in Hockessin

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Near the coast around Hockessin, some shallow wells pick up a salty taste, which can corrode pipes and water heaters faster. Hot water speeds it up. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in Hockessin, DE

    Many poly-era homes also have a blue or black polybutylene service line running from the water meter to the house. Buried poly service lines split, run up bills and saturate the yard.
