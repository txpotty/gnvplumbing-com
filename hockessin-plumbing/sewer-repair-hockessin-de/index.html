    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Hockessin, DE

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Sandy soil drains quickly but can shift and settle, which may stress buried sewer lines and water service pipes over time. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.
