    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Hockessin, DE

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Hockessin

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Hockessin, DE

    Fine grinding with slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. The trap comes off, the tube and arm get cleared with a properly sized cable, and we confirm the arm drops about a quarter inch per foot.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Hockessin, DE

    InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen each use different mounts, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. We stock the common kits. It means most repairs need only one trip.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Hockessin

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Hockessin

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Then the object comes out with pliers. Long stalls can overheat the motor, so we run it under load before we finish.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Hockessin

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. Loose mounting rings rattle too. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Hockessin

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. We check the breaker, the reset button and the switch for silent units, and the flywheel and motor windings for humming ones, then decide if a repair makes sense.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Hockessin, DE

    When the drip starts at the top, the putty under the sink flange is usually dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. That fixes most top leaks. Side leaks are usually the discharge gasket or the dishwasher hose clamp.
