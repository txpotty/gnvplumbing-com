    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Hockessin, DE

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor.     Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Hockessin

    Coastal storms, nor'easters, and the occasional hurricane can flood low-lying parts of Delaware and overwhelm drainage and sewer systems.     Water in Delaware ranges from moderately soft to hard depending on the source, and groundwater areas often see more scale on fixtures.
