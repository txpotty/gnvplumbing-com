    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Hockessin

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. We pump the basin down and scoop out the sediment by hand. A clean pit lets the float move freely. The pump runs cooler too.

    Float Switch Inspection in Hockessin, DE

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Hockessin, DE

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm. Our plumber clears the screen, tests the float and traces the discharge to daylight or an approved drain, since a line that ends under the house only relocates the water. Freezing weather arrives most winters in Delaware, and pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls, and unheated garages are the first to freeze.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Hockessin, DE

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. Each pump gets tested, along with the alternator and the high-water alarm.     Wayne Sump Pump Service in Hockessin, DE

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Loose discharge fittings show up on every brand. Easy fix.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Hockessin

    A high water table means heavy rain can push groundwater into crawlspaces and basements, making a reliable sump pump important. A few weeks ahead of the wet season, we run a hose into the pit until the float trips and the pump starts. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. That leaves time to replace a weak pump before the first big storm, not during it.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Hockessin, DE

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. The float rod gets checked for bends, and the stops get checked for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.
