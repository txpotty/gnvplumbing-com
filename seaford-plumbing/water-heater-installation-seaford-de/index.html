    Expansion Tank Installation in Seaford, DE

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and pipes.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Seaford, DE

    Tankless water heaters provide on-demand hot water, saving energy and space, and they are more and more popular in Delaware homes. Our plumbers specialize in installing these highly efficient units, making sure of proper gas line sizing or electrical service upgrades if needed. We also address venting requirements, which are often different from traditional units. While the upfront cost can be higher, the long-term energy savings and endless hot water supply make them an appealing option for many homeowners in Seaford. We'll help you choose the right model for your specific needs.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Seaford, DE

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Seaford, DE

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, reliable and quiet.
