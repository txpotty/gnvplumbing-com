    Expansion Tank Installation in Seaford, DE

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Seaford, DE

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Seaford, DE

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Gas tankless installs often run higher because of the bigger gas line and new venting, and attic jobs, relocations or code upgrades add labor on top of that. Your quote lists permits. You also get an estimate of yearly energy cost for each option.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Seaford, DE

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Seaford

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. A leak lands on drywall. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Seaford

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. Sizing is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow label, which tells you how much hot water the tank can deliver during a busy hour. Either fuel works. The fuel hookup, the venting and haul-away are all part of the job.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Seaford, DE

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. They do not need a vent. That makes placement easier.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Seaford

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. We follow them exactly. That protects your coverage.
