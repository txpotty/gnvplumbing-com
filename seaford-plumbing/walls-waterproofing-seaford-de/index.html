    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Seaford

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. An encapsulated crawl space can noticeably improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home.     Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Seaford

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. Much of Delaware sits on sandy coastal plain soil with a high water table, which affects slab, crawlspace, and drainage design.     Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Seaford, DE

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Seaford
