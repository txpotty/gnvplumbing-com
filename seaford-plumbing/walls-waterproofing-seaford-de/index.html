    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Seaford, DE

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Seaford

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Vertical cracks are usually a one-visit job.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Seaford, DE

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Seaford
