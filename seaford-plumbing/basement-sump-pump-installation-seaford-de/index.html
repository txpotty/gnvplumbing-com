    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Seaford

    Pedestal sump pumps are an excellent choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level.     Watch for a pump that runs constantly, cycles on and off rapidly, makes grinding noises or never seems to turn on. Rust stains, a stuck float and water sitting above the switch level are also red flags.

    GNV Plumbing offers a comprehensive warranty on our basement sump pump installations, covering both parts and labor. The specific warranty period will be spelled out in your service agreement. We stand behind our work and the quality of the products we install in your Seaford home.

    After flooding, wells should be tested and septic systems checked before normal use, since floodwater can foul both.     Common signs you might need a new sump pump include unusual noises during operation, visible rust or corrosion on the unit, frequent cycling on and off, or a pump that fails to turn on during heavy rain.
