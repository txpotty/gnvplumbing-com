    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Seaford

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. Elevator pit pumps often need oil-detecting controls so hydraulic fluid never gets pumped out with the water. We test duplex systems and high-water alarms on a set schedule. Logs go to the property manager.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Seaford

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. When it fails, the pump pumps the same water twice. Motors wear out faster. Our plumber tests the valve, confirms there is a weep hole below it to prevent air lock, and checks each joint to the wall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Seaford, DE

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. We check the battery with a load tester, not just a voltmeter. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Seaford

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. We pump the basin down and scoop out the sediment by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Seaford, DE

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. Those units need more frequent checks than a house pump. We test the controls, the alarm panel and both pumps in a duplex setup, then log the amp draw on each one so trends show up over time. Downtime costs money.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Seaford, DE

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. We test both pumps, the alternator and the high-water alarm. Property managers get a written log of every reading we take, which helps at insurance renewal and during code inspections. Quarterly visits are common.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Seaford, DE

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Every startup puts heat into the motor. We swap worn valves for quiet, spring-loaded models and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose couplings all the way to the exit point. The water should exit well away from the slab or foundation so it does not soak back toward the pit.

    Submersible Sump Pump Maintenance in Seaford

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. We also clean the intake screen and check the housing for corrosion, which shows up fast on pumps that sit in standing water for years.
