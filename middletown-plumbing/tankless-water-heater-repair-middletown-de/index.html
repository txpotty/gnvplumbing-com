    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Middletown

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, and they hold temperature off thermistor readings. We pull the screen, read sensor values in the service menu and compare thermistor resistance to the chart. Usually under an hour.
