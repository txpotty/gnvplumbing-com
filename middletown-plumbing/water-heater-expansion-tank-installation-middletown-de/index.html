    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Middletown

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and wearing out early.

    Expansion Tank Replacement in Middletown, DE

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a sure sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installation.

Without it, thermal expansion can damage your water heater, pipes and fixtures, leading to leaks and premature system failure. Many local codes in Delaware call for them.

This constant pressure can quickly wear out water heater components and relief valves and even cause leaks or ruptures. The expansion tank absorbs this excess volume, protecting your whole system.
