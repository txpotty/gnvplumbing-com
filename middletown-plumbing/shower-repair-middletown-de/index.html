    Shower Valve Replacement in Middletown

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve. When the body is corroded or the brand stopped making cartridges for it, we replace the valve, usually through a small access panel behind the shower wall so the tile stays put. New valves include pressure balancing. Thermostatic valves are an option if you want exact temperature control.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Middletown, DE

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Middletown

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Middletown, DE

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Then call us. GNV Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Middletown

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. In a single-handle shower the cause is almost always the cartridge or its O-rings, and on an old two-handle valve it is the stem washer and the seat. The parts are on the truck. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Middletown, DE

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. The head comes off first. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Middletown

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. A new cartridge fixes it. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.
