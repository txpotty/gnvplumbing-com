    How We Find Slab Leaks in Middletown, DE

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Middletown, DE

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Middletown

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, listening for the spot where the sound peaks and then checking it again from a different angle, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Middletown, DE

    Hot water leaking under the slab warms the floor above it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.

    Why Locating First Saves Money in Middletown

    Detection usually costs a few hundred dollars. Guessing wrong costs far more.
