    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Middletown, DE

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. A snake punches a hole. High-pressure water strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes them toward the street, and with the right settings it is safe for PVC and older clay alike.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Middletown

    A camera shows the line in real time. Problems become obvious. Our inspection checks for sags, separated joints, roots and corrosion, and we mark each defect on the ground above, with a copy of the video for you.

    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Middletown, DE

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Digging costs far more. If the pipe is structurally sound, regular cleaning is often the better value, but if the camera shows a collapse or a long belly, cleaning only buys time, and you see both prices.

    Tree Root Removal from Sewer Lines in Middletown

    Roots enter through small cracks and loose joints. Sandy soil drains quickly but can shift and settle, which may stress buried sewer lines and water service pipes over time. They grow fast inside. We cut roots out with a saw head, then flush the line with a jetter to clear what the saw leaves behind, and we explain options to keep them out.

    Sewer Line Odor Remediation in Middletown, DE

    Sewer smells mean something is wrong. Gas may be escaping. We clear trapped waste and inspect the line for cracks or separated joints that let sewer gas out.

    Pre-Purchase Sewer Line Cleaning and Inspection in Middletown, DE

    Most home inspections skip the sewer line. It can be costly. We clean the line first so the camera can see the pipe walls clearly, then record the full length and provide a written report that buyers can use to negotiate repairs or credits.
