    Frequently Asked Questions About Urinal Installation in Middletown

    Replacing a urinal on existing lines typically costs $500 to $1,200 including a manual flush valve. New installs with carriers and new drain lines usually range from $1,500 to $3,500, and sensor valves add $250 to $600.

    While it might seem like a way to save money, urinal installation involves precise plumbing connections, proper venting, and secure mounting that are best handled by a professional. Incorrect installation can lead to leaks, odors, and costly repairs down the line, making professional service a worthwhile investment.

    A replacement takes two to four hours. A new install with rough-in and carrier usually takes one to two days, plus inspection time.

    A washdown urinal uses a simple flushing action to rinse the bowl, relying on gravity and the slope of the drain. A siphonic urinal creates a siphonic action during flushing, which pulls waste more strongly down the drain, offering more effective waste removal and usually better odor control.

    Flush urinals use a water flush to remove waste, while waterless urinals use a specialized trap system with a sealant liquid to prevent odors without water. Waterless urinals offer significant water savings but require specific maintenance for their sealant cartridges. Both types offer effective waste disposal.

    GNV Plumbing installs a broad variety of reputable urinal brands, including American Standard, Kohler, Zurn, Sloan, and Toto. We are familiar with the specific requirements of most major manufacturers and can also advise on the best brand for your specific needs and budget.

    Signs that your existing urinal might need replacement include frequent clogs, persistent odors despite cleaning, visible cracks or extensive staining, or if it's an older, inefficient model that uses excessive water. Our technicians can assess its condition and explain repair versus replacement.

    Regular cleaning with appropriate restroom cleaners is essential. For waterless urinals, follow manufacturer guidelines for cartridge replacement. For flushed urinals, make sure the flush mechanism is working correctly and address any slow draining issues promptly to prevent blockages.
