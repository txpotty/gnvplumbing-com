    Standard Hot Water Tank Tune-Up in Middletown

    GNV Plumbing offers a complete tune-up for conventional hot water tanks, focusing on critical components. This includes draining the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is crucial wherever water carries minerals, and inspecting the anode rod for signs of corrosion.     Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Middletown

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Middletown, DE

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing hard. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can ruin a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Burner and Pilot Light Maintenance in Middletown, DE

    For gas hot water tanks, proper burner and pilot light operation is essential for efficient heating. Our service includes cleaning the burner assembly to remove dust and debris that can impede combustion. We also check and adjust the pilot light, making sure it ignites reliably. This attention to detail prevents common issues like intermittent hot water or complete heating failure, which can be stressful for homeowners in Middletown, DE.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Middletown, DE

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Middletown

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout.
