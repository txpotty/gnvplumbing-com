    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Middletown, DE

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Middletown, DE homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes.     Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Middletown

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.
