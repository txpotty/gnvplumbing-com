    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Middletown, DE

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Middletown, DE

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. Finishes get matched when the part exists. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Middletown

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount bowls hang from clips epoxied to the stone, and pedestal sinks rely on a wall bracket that may have pulled out of old drywall over the years. We reset the clips and reseal the rim. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Middletown

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down all day, and nobody checks the trap until water is on the carpet. A strainer basket goes in after we clear it. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Middletown, DE

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A supply line that bursts at night can put several gallons a minute on the floor, so we answer after hours and send a plumber with the parts to stop it. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Middletown, DE

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Code calls for a trap seal between 2 and 4 inches, and a trap that leaks, sags or was cut down by a past owner can lose that seal and let the smell in. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Middletown

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. If the angle stop will not stop it, the main shutoff will, and the sooner it is closed the less water ends up inside the vanity floor and the subfloor below. Then lay towels down. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Middletown, DE

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We run a camera or a longer cable into the wall arm and look for a sagging line or buildup where the sink ties into the tub drain. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.
