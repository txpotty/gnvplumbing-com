    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Middletown, DE: Style by Style

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Middletown

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Middletown

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Mounting style changes the plumbing. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Middletown, DE

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks each have their own health code rules. Prep sinks usually need an indirect drain to a floor sink with an air gap, while the three-compartment sink often runs through the grease trap. The local rule gets checked first. We work before opening or after close so service is not interrupted.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Middletown

    Cheap stainless bowls are noisy when dishes hit them or the disposal runs. Better sinks come with rubber pads and an undercoating, and if yours does not, we can add a coat of spray undercoating before it goes in. It is a small upgrade. Once the bowl is mounted, the space underneath is hard to reach, so this is a job worth doing while the sink is still on the floor.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Middletown, DE

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Middletown, DE

    A fireclay apron sink sits on a frame inside the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. We plan the drain early. A lot of them drain toward the back, so the trap layout has to match.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Middletown

    Drop-in sinks are common in Delaware homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim carries the weight. We check the cutout, run a bead of silicone under the lip and tighten the mounting clips evenly around the bowl so the rim seats flat. Next we connect and test the faucet and drain.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Middletown, DE

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. We mount the disposal, run the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop and install a dedicated hot supply with its own shutoff. One visit costs less than three. Every connection is tested under real use.
